We regret to report that one of our game masters (GMs) has been dismissed from duty based on the fact that they leaked information.

The GM in question used both their GM account and their private personal account to forward a specific player's conversation logs to a third party.

Based on information reported by customers, an internal investigation confirmed the facts above. The GM was dismissed from duty as of today, their personal PlayOnline account was forcibly terminated, and they resigned from the company the same day of their own volition.

Leaking information that only a GM could know must never happen, and we deeply apologize that conduct occurred which betrayed our customers' trust.

To prevent any recurrence, we will strengthen auditing and management of GM action logs and thoroughly educate staff on information handling, and we will strive to provide better service.
