Hello, this is Fujito. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 67," with guest voice actress Emiri Kato, centered on the November version update and Vana'Bout. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (November/December) (00:09:39): all revivals - November: Volume 1 Poroggos, Volume 2 Heartwings; December: Volume 1 Soulflayers, Volume 2 Flans. No battle adjustments - recall your old tactics. December's Flans, spanning the new year, bring the Golden Bomb as an otoshidama - a good chance for Hallmarks.
  • System updates (00:11:58): PadConfig can now be launched while the game is running with changes applying immediately - no more quitting the game to adjust gamepad settings. While PadConfig is open, the "enable pad while game is inactive" feature is disabled, so configure somewhere safe like your Mog House. On Windows 11: verification is under way and will take a little longer; when the official site is updated to list support, the site itself will also be overhauled.
  • Master Trials (00:17:15): the next Master Trial is planned for February 2024, a battle featuring the Serpent Generals... rather, the seven Sin Hunters including Ogurost and the Undying Lord - somewhat harder than the recently implemented trial, so prepare.
  • Vana'Bout (00:21:48): a new campaign coming soon, designed so you can feel you have comrades across worlds - everyone cooperates to build up points via dedicated Records of Eminence, and reaching the target unlocks rewards benefiting all worlds. The first run, announced on air for late November, moves to December because the special-site integration needs more development time - apologies for the wait.
  • New battlefield: "Rondo in Pink" (桃色輪舞曲) (00:36:35): available from the November update. Its theme and the behind-the-scenes story of its homage source are told in the video; entry conditions are in the update notes.

Announcements (00:44:38)

  • November Login Campaign: headline item Chocobo Chair II, a recolor of the existing Chocobo Chair.
  • Month of Eleven Growth Campaign: campaigns tied to the ever-popular Nyzul Isle Uncharted Area Survey and A.M.A.N. Trove.
  • A.M.A.N. Trove Campaign: two silver Mutual Aid vouchers at first daily login, from November 20.
  • Winter Trust Campaign: December 1 - January 8.
  • New Year's Nomad Mog Bonanza 2024: from December 16 17:00, two tickets per character; new vanity equipment planned for rank 3.
  • Starlight Celebration: from December 16 17:00.
  • Welcome Back & Discount: Welcome Back December 22 - January 9 - a perfect homecoming after visiting the exhibition. (Correction: the Discount campaign, stated on air as through January 31, ends January 18.)
  • WE ARE VANA'DIEL Producer Sessions, Season 2: interviews with development members from FFXI's middle years to the present - #1: Chains of Promathia - Nobuaki Komoto & Koichi Ogawa and #2: Treasures of Aht Urhgan - Ogawa, Mizuki Ito & Yosuke Hayashi are up, with more to come.
  • Echoes of Vana'diel: to celebrate FFXI's 20th anniversary, the FFXIV team is creating crossover content - same business division, same MMORPG genre, lifting each other up. Planning is under way; watch for FFXIV-side announcements.
  • The Hidden Treasures of Vana'diel in Osaka (01:08:20): from December 22, 2023 at Namba Marui; dates, tickets, and new venue goods (several surprising items) on the official site and official X.
  • Stage reading "FFXI: The Songstress of Another Tale" (異聞のウタイビト): schedule and additional cast announced - see the official site and official X.
