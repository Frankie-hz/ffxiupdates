Hello, this is Fujito. Thank you for watching the 72nd Freshly Picked Vana'diel, with voice actress Emiri Kato as guest, covering the May version update and upcoming plans. The broadcast opened with "The Adventure Continues.", a 24th-anniversary animation by animator Waboku, who joined to talk about depicting one adventurer gaining new companions and moving forward, and shared their own Vana'diel memories.

May version update

Official site renewal: the site moved to https, reorganized its menus, and enlarged banners for mobile readability.

Tutorial quests (01:01:49): renewed after 10+ years - conditions and rewards modernized, with paths into Records of Eminence and Trust acquisition, warp and Survival Guide introductions, and a flow leading to obtaining a support job. Clear status was reset for everyone. Rewards now include the Echad Ring (experience bonus) and the Dejon... Return Ring (warp), among others.

Records of Eminence (01:03:48): growth-related objectives now auto-accept, guiding players through merit point unlocks and the level 99 cap increases; objectives name the relevant quest NPCs. Players who already meet conditions clear them on acceptance and receive Abdhaljs seals and coupons exchangeable for one piece of AF119+3, and even relic or empyrean 119+3 - worthwhile for veterans too.

Ambuscade (01:06:02): Hallmark and Gallantry gains increased, especially in Chapter 2, to reduce repetition for solo players; the cumulative Hallmark rewards add a "Trust manual" granting Trust points, exchangeable monthly.

Trust system (01:08:41): March added Trust points, allowing each parameter to be raised through 50 stages (gains vary by Trust type - mage Trusts gain less HP, more MP). May added "combat skill" and "magic skill" as reinforcement categories, also 50 stages; some weapon skills now reference combat skill, with accuracy adjustments (see the May update notes). Morimar's jobs were swapped to Warrior/Beastmaster so his great axe actually has a skill backing it, changing his traits and raising his damage. Trust points come from Records of Eminence and more sources are in preparation.

Limbus (01:16:52): weekly "Temenos codes" and "Apollyon codes" (from examining "???") shorten the chest-opening grind; codes keep until used, so they can be combined across weeks.

Anniversary event (01:20:26): "The Discerning Adventurer Development Program" - transform into a moogle and hunt up to 16 out-of-place objects in the three starting cities. Points exchange for a Trust textbook (one per character), daily Mutual Aid vouchers (silver), dial keys #ANV and #FES, and more; the Shade Mog Rod (HQ obtainable through points) carries a transformation enchantment.

Vana'bout: the title-award bug (top-10 Prowess without a ranking-board listing yielding no title) was fixed. The 24th-anniversary May round is a relaxed "Yuru-bout": looser personal rewards (Limit Breaker at 600+ Prowess), a new silver-prize "Coupon I-Amb" exchangeable for Ambuscade weapon/armor upgrade materials and tickets, a 100%-only overall goal, 2 daily and 6 period objectives, and new objectives designed to complete alongside normal play.

June version update

Limbus: the planned update series completes - new Notorious Monsters with newly designed equipment, plus two new BGM arrangements (one heard in the anniversary animation) playing when the new NMs appear.

Campaigns

(01:43:04) May: the weekly-expanding "24th Anniversary Thank You Campaign"; a login campaign headlined by the Katakama... (single-sickle spear) plus Prishe's Boots, Shantotto II, and AF upgrade materials; the Moogle newcomer/returnee campaign distributing Mog Pells (red and Ochre) and a Trust manual to everyone; the 24th Anniversary Nomad Mog Bonanza with shortened sale/draw windows and Thruda... (Sroda) gear as the second prize - the Sroda Necklace boosts status-resistance magic (+20; double base for area bar-spells, 1.6x for single-target) party-wide when the caster wears it; the renewed "Thank You Adventurers" lineup (new Jubilee Ring with skill-gain and experience bonuses; Mog Pell silver/red weeks); the Echoes of Vana'diel discount for FFXIV players starting FFXI at half price; the FFXIV crossover finale "Windurst: The Third Walk"; Eorzea Cafe tie-in menus from June 30; the in-FFXI "Re-investigate the Anomalies of Vana'diel" event, round 2 featuring Alexander with materials upgrading high-grade race armor (now body and legs in addition to hands and feet); "The 'Real' Archduke of Jeuno" rerun featuring Kam'lanaut's sword and shield; and the ongoing Welcome Back and Discount campaigns.

Future plans

Free trial overhaul (02:26:31): planned change from 14 days to unlimited duration, with the level cap raised from 50 to 75 - making the Shadow Lord reachable - while keeping expansion areas, auction, trade, and gil restrictions. Timing undecided but targeted as soon as possible.

New content (02:31:52): a yearly large addition is planned - currently a concept where players fight Notorious Monsters placed in the field, with rewards bridging the gap between Ambuscade gear and Malignance gear, aimed at mid-level and solo-friendly progression.

Trust system: next comes Trust-dedicated equipment - conceived like Puppetmaster attachments rather than bag-consuming player gear, obtainable from shops, the new content's drops, and other varied sources; more detail in the November broadcast.

High-tier mission battlefields (02:39:11): the three add-on scenarios - A Crystalline Prophecy, A Moogle Kupo d'Etat, and A Shantotto Ascension - will get battlefields with rewards; "A Crystalline Prophecy" is targeted for this summer, the others later. Clearing each add-on (sold individually or via Vana'diel Collection 4) is required. Rewards are the scenario equipment remade with fixed IL119 stats instead of selectable augments.

Development environment (02:43:20): FFXI's asset pipeline still dated from PS2 development hardware; the environment is being modernized. The server replacement is complete - FFXI now has a secure environment for at least five more years, with virtualization letting one server host multiple areas and easing recovery. A PlayOnline Viewer patch for modern PCs is the top priority (the cause is known; building the patch pipeline takes time), which delays some UI conveniences like blue magic and Puppetmaster set functions. Area IDs are exhausted, but engineers believe repurposed reserved space can yield new area slots - investigation continues.
