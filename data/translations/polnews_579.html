We investigated the unexpected problem that forced the event's cancellation and identified the cause. We again apologize to everyone who participated last time.

The problem: the event target, the "mysterious giant creature," had partially faulty parameter and behavior data, and under specific conditions could suddenly be defeated with most of its HP remaining.

We fixed this and also increased the number of places where participation rights can be obtained in the target areas.

Event restart: Saturday, August 30, 2003, around 19:00 (JST)

Participation notes:

  • The event runs from around 19:00 Saturday, August 30 to around 24:00 Sunday, August 31. Defeating the creature within this period means success; what happens as a result will be announced after the event.
  • When many players attack, the flood of battle messages can slow the client. Turning ON chat filters like "others' attacks" and "others' missed attacks" reduces the load; the filtering happens server-side, so it also reduces network load. We recommend keeping filters for unneeded messages ON in general.
  • Participation rights last about 30 minutes. Being KO'd without them loses experience points.
  • See the previous announcement for other conditions and cautions.
