Regarding WebMoney refunds accompanying the free April billing period: refunds for customers who suspended billing at the end of March 2011 and resumed during April began Friday, May 27, 2011 at 21:30 (JST).

Together with guidance for customers already covered since Thursday, April 28, 12:00, here is how to confirm your refund.

Customers who paid the April monthly fee with WebMoney: from Thursday, April 28, 12:00 (no end date has been set; it will be announced when decided). Confirm via the PlayOnline Viewer under Service & Support, Member Information, "WebMoney Refund". Eligible customers see a prepaid number and amount under the "April refund" item. Customers with a prorated refund portion may see two prepaid numbers.

Customers who suspended billing at the end of March 2011 and resumed during April: from Friday, May 27, 21:30 (no end date has been set). Confirm the same way; eligible customers see prepaid numbers and amounts under the "April refund (reactivation)" item. Up to three prepaid numbers may be shown depending on the number of eligible content IDs.
