Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 42." We broadcast ahead of the November version update, covering the update itself plus a rundown of the KADOKAWA-hosted "FINAL FANTASY XI FAN EVENT 2018 #FF11 Message." Summary below.

Version Update Highlights (0:02:27)

  • Ambuscade (0:02:44): New weapons added to the Hallmark/Gallantry exchange lineup. The concept: weapons you can strengthen by steadily playing Ambuscade and keep using until you obtain endgame-grade weapons. Further reinforcement stages are planned. Reinforcement materials come from Hallmark/Gallantry exchange; each stage uses a different material (rather than stockpiling one material across stages) to avoid inventory pressure - exchange what you need when you need it. (0:13:08) This month's opponent was kept secret for the Morbol Idol Election, so we only showed the graphics of the Expert-tier equipment.
  • Beastmaster adjustments (0:13:59): Job Point contents changed. Since Job Master status became required for reinforcing relic/mythic/ergon/empyrean/aeonic weapons, and "Pet Enmity Boost" was constraining beastmasters' options in party play, we made an urgent change separate from the planned job adjustments. On job adjustments generally: we are in discussions with the designers, surveying the state of each job. The policy is to first adjust elements that broadly affect all jobs (such as weapon types) rather than jobs themselves - not overhauls that change how a job plays, nor sharpening already-sharp edges, but raising damage where "this much power is fine" while keeping each job's identity.

Roadmap and Campaigns (0:19:56)

  • Welcome Back & Discount Campaigns: Welcome Back through ~17:00 Nov 20; Discount through ~17:00 Nov 30.
  • Autumn Growth Campaign: two phases - first, generous experience/capacity point support for returnees from the Welcome Back campaign; second, a long-running double synthesis skill-up campaign (normally limited to one or two days). Details on this topics page.
  • Try Your Luck with A.M.A.N. Trove: Nov 9 through ~23:00 Nov 23; logging in grants one silver Mutual Aid voucher per day, up to 30 total.
  • Adventurer Appreciation Campaign: Nov 12 through ~23:59 Dec 30; log in once a week for up to four kinds of growth-support items - returnees especially should grab these.
  • New Year's Dream Mog Bonanza 2019: the first prize is a voucher for a relic/mythic/ergon/empyrean/aeonic weapon with every currently implemented reinforcement applied, plus the new vanity item Karakul Cap. From the December update, Bonanza vanity prizes (including existing ones) become deliverable to characters on the same account - and Bonanza Balls become edible, among other delightful changes. Sale periods and drawing dates on the topics page.
  • 16th Anniversary Present Campaign (0:35:53): to mark "XI Day," a shirt printed with the Shadow Lord's silhouette, delivered to your inventory just for logging in.
  • November Login Campaign: headline item is the ♪Goobbue mount (shown in the broadcast).
  • Bonds and Encounters Screenshot Campaign: a "bonds"-themed screenshot campaign is planned; the prize is the R. Chocobo Cap (R for "Royal").
  • Absolute Virtue event (0:47:54): the third in-game event drafting an idle boss - following "The Archduke Wants Attention Again" and "Shadow Lord Day" - featuring Absolute Virtue. Some reward weapons were previewed.
  • Rhapsodies of Vana'diel: online streaming planned from Friday, November 30; details to follow.
