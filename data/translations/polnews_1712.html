Regarding the error displays during WebMoney payment that occurred on Wednesday, March 31, 2004, here are the results of the investigation by WebMoney Corporation and ourselves.

The cause was confirmed to be processing delays from excessive load on WebMoney's payment servers, caused by a sudden, large volume of WebMoney payment requests around that time.

WebMoney is currently mitigating by adjusting the number of simultaneous payment processes, but reports that a fundamental fix will take some time (details under investigation). Until the problem is finally resolved, the same phenomenon may occur at the end of each month. If you use WebMoney, please avoid month-end where possible for content ID purchases and period extensions, to help distribute the load.

For customers whose WebMoney points were deducted without the extension or purchase completing, we are arranging refunds of the points in turn; this takes several days.
