• The new white magic spell Teleport-Vahzl (teleport to Xarcabard) and black magic spell Escape (emergency exit to a dungeon's entrance) have been added.
  • The reuse time of the beastmaster ability Charm has been greatly shortened.
  • The food effects (attack/defense boosts) of the following items have been changed from fixed values to percentages:
    • Defense: nebimonite, gold lobster, fish mithkabob, land crab meat, boiled crab, nebimonite bake, shall shell, crayfish, boiled crayfish
    • Attack: grilled hare, dried meat, meat mithkabob, roast mutton, dhalmel steak, pipira, salted pipira
  • Bonuses have been added to the stats of the following equipment: Mage's Sandals, Temple Knight's Collar (神殿騎士団カラー), Royal Knight's Collar (王立騎士団カラー), Mage's Robe, Federation Strategist's Staff (連邦軍師制式錫).
  • The following items have been added as drops from elementals of each element; using one turns it into 12 crystals: fire cluster, ice cluster, wind cluster, earth cluster, lightning cluster, water cluster, light cluster, dark cluster.
  • When a party (or alliance) contains a member of level 51 or higher, experience division is now based on current level ratios rather than the ratio of required experience, easing the experience penalty in parties with level gaps.
  • Fixes for problems from the previous version update:
    • Bazaar comments disappearing or moving to other characters
    • System messages from Check not being filtered
    • 16:9 screenshots being corrupted
    • Freezes caused by the [items] completion dictionary
    • Battle message timing abnormalities
    • Bugs following the ranged attack changes: 1) Flaming Arrow and Hot Shot damaging the attacker; 2) weapon skills being accepted with no ammunition equipped; 3) Barrage's effect being lost during auto-attack
    • Musketeer Gun (銃士制式長銃) equip level corrected from 51 to 50
    • Incorrect icons for flax and several other items
    • Cross Counter data error corrected (D+29 was wrong; correct is D+19)
    • Mage's Ballad score mislabeled as a warriors' score
    • Ranger's Eagle Eye Shot miss message corrected
  • Magic Burst activation is now provisionally announced by a message. Packet cleanup continues so it can be shown more clearly in the future.
  • The pronoun <tp> has been added, displaying your current TP%.
  • While under Invisible, Hide, Camouflage, and similar effects, your own character is now shown semi-transparent so you can see yourself; other players still cannot see you at all.
  • Logging areas added: East Ronfaure and Buburimu Peninsula, with hatchets now sold in Mhaura. Logging yields more item types, with per-area specialties, and hatchets break less often.
  • Harvesting now yields Sarutabaruta cotton, flax, and other items more often, and sickles break less often.
  • Treasure chests can now sometimes yield gil instead of items; higher-level dungeons yield more gil, split evenly among party members.
  • When a thief fails to open a chest with thief's tools or a living key, the trap activation chance has been slightly increased. The chance of opening is unchanged.
  • The trigger chance of the Mog Safe expansion quest (when its conditions are met) has been doubled.
  • In Conquest, beastman-control borderlines in the following regions are now set lower than in ordinary regions - beastman stronghold regions: Norvallen, Aragoneu, Derfland, Valdeaunia; semi-stronghold regions: Qufim, Fauregandi.
  • New conquest reward items: the nation rings "San d'Orian Ring," "Bastokan Ring," and "Windurstian Ring" are available for individual conquest points, exchangeable only by rank 3+ adventurers while their nation is in 1st place. The starting nation rings are now Rare.
  • During alliance campaigns, Outpost merchants now also trade with adventurers from allied nations.
  • The special areas holding boss monsters can now be searched (previously impossible): Behemoth's Dominion, Monastic Cavern, Qu'Bia Arena, Altar Room. (These cannot yet be specified with /sea.)
  • Check during battle now uses sub-target selection like magic.
  • Due to population growth, a new world will be opened this month; details to follow. For this reason there will be no monthly special event this month.
