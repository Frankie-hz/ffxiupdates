Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 64," covering the February and March version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (February/March) (00:02:23): both revivals - Volume 1 Khaos (コース), Volume 2 Demons. Khaos changes: when the targeted player is beyond a certain distance, it now uses "Gambol Macabre" (ガンボルマカブル); within that distance the move is used only while its skeleton attendants are up; and activating its SP ability Blood Weapon now clears status ailments from itself and its attendants. The Demon fight is unadjusted - it's about choosing kill priority, and area magic may give you an edge. Volume 1 adjustment details here. March: Volume 1 is Sahagin, adjusted as needed; Volume 2 will be a spring-themed monster.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (00:08:42): no story in February, but two more "Defeat the Curious Monsters!" Records of Eminence objectives. The climactic arc eleven begins in March; because the story and new scenery are a large workload, the first half arrives in March and the second half in May.
  • Sortie (February) (00:13:10): investigation found server processing degrades badly once the layer areas generated per base area exceed a threshold, so base areas were increased from two to three. With this, the temporarily removed auto-entry feature returns (your previous settings are preserved). The reservation system update announced January 27 needs work at a fundamental level and is postponed for safety; its aim is to reduce queue-jumping by guiding reservations into vacant layer areas in reservation order rather than lining players up per layer.
  • Other updates (00:21:11): Pets called by Dragoon/Beastmaster/Summoner/Puppetmaster scaled stats with the master's equipment level, but physical and magic evasion were suppressed - now raised in light of current conditions; the critical damage cap on physical special moves/auto-attacks was also raised at all levels (it was being hit, making upgrades imperceptible). Pets charmed with Charm are excluded. Level-difference correction removed in Xarcabard and Newton Movalpolos. Term Filter: hides chat lines containing player-registered terms - place "prohibited.txt" (terms to filter) and "allowed.txt" (terms always shown) in the designated folder (these files are excluded from version updates so your entries aren't overwritten; you create them yourself), then enable the term filter in the in-game config for immediate effect, even while the game runs. Applies to Shout/Yell/AssistJ/AssistE. Setup was demonstrated on video; details also in the update notes. Regular expression support is planned in future updates. Copy to clipboard: copies chat log lines (other than system messages) to the PC clipboard, including timestamps if displayed; auto-translate brackets and game-only symbols are omitted.
