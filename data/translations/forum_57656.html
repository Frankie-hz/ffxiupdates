Odyssey (January/February): Sheol Gaol

  • Overview: "Gaol" is the old spelling of "jail." Sheol Gaol is content where you defeat NMs under special conditions and use points earned in battle to strengthen equipment. Participation requires certain levels and Records of Eminence completion. 1-6 players; 15 minutes per NM battle; up to 3 NM battles per entry.
  • Entry requirements: (1) complete the Records of Eminence objective "Reach Sheol C"; (2) hold three of the key item "Mog Packet II" (each exchangeable for 3,000 Mog Segments); (3) have three jobs at level 99.
  • Flow: enter from Rabao via the familiar veridical conflux; Mog Packet IIs come from the Pilgrim Moogle. A staging area lets you change jobs and access the Mog Safe, satchel, storage furniture, and wardrobes (no delivery post). In the boss area you can call Trusts; defeating the NM returns you to the staging area.
  • Party rule: parties must additionally satisfy "members x 3 distinct level-99 jobs with no job duplicated" - several non-qualifying examples were illustrated in the attachments.
  • Equipment: defeating an NM grants a corresponding key item; while holding it, buy the equipment from the Pilgrim Moogle with gil (no direct drops). Fully reinforced pieces gain the augments shown in the attachment.
  • Reinforcement: meeting certain conditions unlocks equipment reinforcement and the Vengeance (difficulty) setting. NMs at Veng+1 or higher yield Reinforcement Points (RP), scaled by difficulty and performance; even on a time-out you earn RP for HP removed, with full kills earning more. At Veng+1+, while you hold occupancy you can re-engage an NM at its reduced HP. RP can also be converted into Mog Segments.
  • Coming up: from February, new NMs and matching equipment will be added together.

Ambuscade (January/February)

January: Volume 1 is a Medusa revival; Volume 2, for the Year of the Ox, is a Buffalo - a simple single-enemy fight designed around its ponderous, weighty gait. February: Volume 1 is an Iron Giant with a new graphic; Volume 2 is in development, and new vanity equipment is planned.
