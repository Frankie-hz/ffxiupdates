Hello, this is Fujito. Thank you for watching the 71st Freshly Picked Vana'diel, with voice actress Emiri Kato as guest, covering the November version update and the future of FFXI. A summary for those who missed the broadcast:

Year 23 policy review

(00:04:48) The "enriching individual-scale activity" policy was reviewed: not solo play per se, but letting each adventurer pursue what they want without being bound by party-size requirements.

Trust reinforcement (00:06:12): first the base system was prepared by expanding acquisition (Trusts added to login campaign lineups, SNS guides, Mog Pell (Ochre) distribution). Going forward, Trust strength will be raised so more content is viable with them. The plan under consideration: allocate reinforcement points per type; a first phase focusing on the base, and a second adding customization on top. Rather than farming battle content, reinforcement is intended to come through daily logins and individual activity.

More item acquisition routes (00:16:19): job-attire upgrade materials got more routes and lower rates; the May update relaxed required material counts and put Rem's Tales chapters into login campaigns. October changed the "★The Winged Goddess" battlefield's spoils from Malignance attire to wrapped Malignance packaging so missing pieces are easier to complete. Goblin Stew 880 is now permanently sold (150,000 gil normally; 100,000 during Mog Garden campaigns) for faster bag expansion. Seasonal reward items that had become unobtainable can be re-acquired from the Curator Moogle NPCs, including new items such as Halloween's Illum attire and the Duke's Sword - a seasonal item that unusually carries usable stats, meant to give new and returning players an early practical piece.

Content expansion (00:28:41): Besieged updates continued (new exchange items including the Alzadaal ring warping before the Ashu Talif, and Escutcheon synthesis cards). The renewed Limbus changed from an instanced format to a freely enterable field and became the new upgrade venue for artifact and relic attire; updates continue (visibility of particle-yielding "???" points, floor-indicator UI, monster behavior). Adoulin missions had fame requirements lowered for Rhapsodies pacing, Coalition Imprimatur issuance time roughly halved (helping Ygnas and Ergon weapons), and the time-gated barriers in Yorcia Weald were removed - an April Fools' joke made real.

November version update

Limbus (00:38:55): the NM-kill tally and chest-change period changed from 1 week to 4 weeks in October; November adds a visible refresh timestamp. The hidden "lucky chest" mechanic was disclosed: every player always has exactly one winning chest assigned, and it does not move until opened - so if you get 3,000 particles, try opening chests on other floors.

Login campaign renewal (00:45:40): daily point amounts, carryover, and lineups were reworked. Rewards are now spread finely from 5 to 1,500 points; the carryover cap becomes 700 points; logins 1-14 give 50 points with a bonus on the 15th login.

Battlefield "Glimmer of Magatama... (the Rhapsodies story battle)" (00:56:30): players who already cleared it and join as helpers now receive, on victory, a 100%-drop personal reward granting Exemplar Points - please help adventurers stuck on Rhapsodies progression.

Jugner Forest [S] (01:03:03): the "carved wooden poles" are now visible on screen, making them harder to miss in this one-way-heavy area.

Dynamis - Divergence (01:05:37): existing entrances are renamed "#1" and new "#2" entrances open "shared" Divergence areas enterable solo (previously 3+ players required). Shared areas allow one entry per Earth day, show remaining enemies and wave before entry, use claim rules like classic Dynamis, have no Trust limit, and allow forming parties inside (or entering pre-formed, with everyone examining the entrance). Relic attire +3 reforging becomes far more accessible. Per-nation opening schedules differ, and you can do both the shared and instanced versions.

Vana'bout (01:19:30): daily objectives can now be cancelled/skipped; objectives carry detailed NPC/location/clear-condition text (rolling out gradually); new objectives added; period objectives raised from 7 to 9; and the silver prize adds "Coupon A-AF+3", exchangeable for a full set of any job's AF119+3. Round 7's 150% reward adds a campaign making the 100-monster kill count easier alongside the statue campaigns.

A.M.A.N. Trove (01:26:51): spoils renewed with entirely new items (retired items listed in the November update notes); Venus Orb rates raised, with the three "sound" tiers now audible. New spoils include treasure-hunter-boosting items, gil-fed enchantments, and the debut of "Knowledge Rank" - a value reflecting how many missions, quests, and pieces of content you have cleared, with effects scaling to the rank; more activities were added to the rank's coverage.

Congested worlds (01:56:36): Asura and Bahamut remain closed to new characters; server trouble has settled and decongestion measures are being considered.

Schedule (01:59:24): November - login campaign (headline item at 150 points, upgrade materials included), the split Equipment Upgrade & Synthesis Support Campaign with a special "Odyssey: Aim for the Goal" campaign, Vana'bout 7, and "The 'Real' Archduke of Jeuno Wants Attention Too - Part 3" featuring Kam'lanaut's shield via the Curator Moogle. December - the New Year's Nomad Mog Bonanza 2026 with a mount as a new first prize, and the Starlight Celebration with new items.

Future updates

(02:05:20) Limbus is about 60% implemented; December adds powered-up returns of Ultima and Omega with new looks (in-lore, Limbus manufactures ever-more-advanced lifeforms). Besieged approaches its top rank: CL119 planned for January, with the Serpent Generals and surrounding NPCs strengthened, and new exchange items later. Colonization/Lair Reives: the unintended-participation penalty is being reviewed for removal. Magic map prices will be lowered and made purchasable in one place. December's Starlight Celebration adds a new dial to the Goblin Mystery Box, planned to appear in future seasonal events as well.
