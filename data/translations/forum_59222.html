Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 59," covering the February and March version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Dusky/Divine Might medicine compensation: a reminder that the compensation period runs through the February version update, including the claim conditions.
  • Ambuscade (February/March) (00:09:15): both brand new - Volume 1 is Khaos (コース), Volume 2 is Taurus. Against the Khaos, deliberately taking its special moves can make for an unusual fight, though you can also simply push through with high damage - your choice. Taurus was chosen as a Setsubun-style ogre: as its HP falls it summons attendants that strengthen it; they can't be killed but can be neutralized for a time. Burn the Taurus down fast, or strip its buffs by handling the attendants first, to suit your party. New reward: the Khaos staff. March - Volume 1 is Antica (revival); Volume 2 is in tuning with a spring theme planned.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (February) (00:13:44): the seventh arc begins - back to Bastok, starting with Gumbah, with the story unfolding on Zepwell Island.
  • Other updates (00:14:44): Master Level cap rises from 20 to 30 (Exemplar Point requirements grow - take it at your own pace). NPCs will no longer accept requiem weapons that lead to already-completed mythic weapons (January made those quests cancelable; now they can't be taken at all). March adds monsters to Crawlers' Nest [S]: previously unopened doors will open, with monsters easier to hunt than November's Promyvion additions.
  • Storage (00:21:00): Mog Wardrobes 5-8 added - subscribe to as many as you want (pricing and sign-up on the special site). Regardless of subscriptions: the title screen now shows wardrobe icons (subscribed ones bright, unsubscribed translucent); the Mog House menu consolidates the safe and wardrobes under a "Storage" button; items can move directly between storages without passing through your bag (use the "switch" button in list options). The much-requested expansions arrive: macro books to 40 and equipment sets to 200. A "Recycle" feature recovers discarded items - up to the last 10, with the history reset by area changes or logout; lost lots, temporary items, and linkshells (except bell sacks, pearls, and unopened linkshells) are excluded. February also changes item loading: items become usable storage by storage as they load, and macros with same-named equipment will equip whichever copy loaded first - possibly the wrong augment - so keep your main gear in Mog Wardrobe 1, which loads first.
