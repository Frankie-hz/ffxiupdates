Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 58," covering the November and December version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (November/December) (0:04:16): November - Volume 1 is Iron Giants, renewed to be more approachable than this February's version: the pattern where two giants act alternately was substantially weakened, Stoneskin removed, and debuff durations shortened. Balancing additions like Regen mean the old approach may feel harder - try the alternating pattern. Volume 2 is Lizards: whichever lizard type outnumbers the other grows stronger, so kill toward equal numbers and it shouldn't be a hard fight. New rewards added. December - Volume 1 is a Moogle revival; Volume 2's main monster is in tuning but we're planning a Golden Bomb appearance. From December we're also considering adjusting exchange quantities for Ambuscade armor reinforcement materials (armor only, not capes).
  • The Voracious Resurgence (November) (0:12:45): the sixth arc begins, centered on Leiqu (レイクウ). Each time a story chapter concludes, a monthly Records of Eminence objective unlocks, granting capacity points and silver Mutual Aid vouchers, replayable at each patch.
  • Trust shield adjustments (0:15:41): shield-bearing Trusts called by a master of item level 100+ gain increased defense and shield block rate. ArkEV's special shield instead grants the defense increase regardless of master item level.
  • Other November changes (0:17:09): The minigame Mandra☆ becomes permanent, with all opponent NPCs available from the start (play and difficulty unchanged; event NPC locations listed in the update notes). Rewards accrue by cumulative chari (ちゃり), claimable up to 300 per month; the reward list can be reset once per version update for 10,000 gil. The quest "Hara-kiri" (ハラキリ) was adjusted: more target fish, centered on large fish from Seekers of Adoulin onward; heavier fish raise item rates; several new obtainable items (some shown on video).
  • Master Levels (0:22:26): a new growth element for Job Masters. Defeating monsters under certain conditions grants Exemplar Points; Master Level starts at 0 and rises at point thresholds, increasing character and pet parameters and combat/magic skill caps - no point allocation needed. Every 5 Master Levels raises the support job level cap by 1. Conditions for earning Exemplar Points: be a Job Master, hold the key item "Master Breaker" (from the Nomad Moogle at Ru'Lude Gardens H-5, on a mastered job), and the target must check as "Easy Prey" or above. Cautions covered: some support-job ability restrictions, and being KO'd costs a portion of Exemplar Points - Master Levels can go down, and experience can be lost. Experience boost effects do NOT increase Exemplar Point gains. The cap is 20 for now, to be raised in stages. Alongside this, monster placement changed in Promyvion-Holla/Mea/Dem/Vahzl and the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins; the gear blocking the Ob pop site (The Wyrmking Descends) will be removed next update.
  • AF119+3 / Relic armor 119+3 reforge prerequisites (0:33:57): new ways to meet the unlock conditions. AF119+3: defeat a set number of specific monsters in Omen. Relic 119+3: previously required per-job mid-boss kills in Divergence; now either that or defeating a set number of specific monsters. Omen monsters can now drop scraps (小札), and Treasure Hunter applies; raising the scrap stack size to 99 is under consideration.
