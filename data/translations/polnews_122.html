Starting with the next run on Saturday, February 1, the method of the monthly processing performed on the 1st of each month by the servers that manage enrollment and cancellation will change.

Under the old behavior, if a paid content ID became unusable because a WebMoney extension was forgotten, or a required payment-method change (credit card, OCN PayOn, @nifty billing) was missed, the content could still be used until the first server maintenance after the monthly processing. From February onward, the connection will instead be terminated automatically during the monthly processing window.

This means that even players in the middle of a play session will be disconnected automatically, and the content cannot be used again until the monthly processing finishes and the content ID is reactivated.

The next monthly processing on Saturday, February 1 will also run longer, from 0:00 to 4:00. WebMoney customers in particular should take care to complete their content ID renewal before the monthly processing begins. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.
