Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 51." The broadcast covered the July and August version updates and more. Summary below.

Current Development Status (00:03:48)

The previously announced new scenario content's first installment is planned for the August version update. It was originally meant to coincide with the 18th anniversary but had to be postponed due to COVID-19. The story will be added gradually; it includes episodes touching on events and people from your adventures so far.

Version Update Highlights

From this broadcast on we cover two months of update information at a time where possible, instead of one.

  • Ambuscade (July/August) (00:15:57): July - Volume 1 is Qiqirn (revival), Volume 2 is Spiders. The Volume 2 boss changes modes over time and by damage dealt; matching its mode - physical against physical, magic against magic - is effective. August's foes are decided but still being tuned; Volume 1 will be new rather than a revival, and vanity equipment is among the rewards.
  • Small job adjustments: Going forward (00:19:57), we'll keep adjusting jobs based on relative balance shifts and performance in current content plus your feedback. The White Mage adjustments taught us how hard it is to make large changes to existing elements, so we're also considering a new growth element for ALL jobs - additive rather than reworking. It's a long-term plan; details when consideration progresses.
    • Paladin (July) (00:23:16): new job trait Shield Barrier; Majesty adjusted; Reactive (リアクト) adjusted; some shields' defense raised. If Paladin simply took over Rune Fencer's role both jobs would lose their identity, so the changes extend Paladin's strength: defense. Specifics in the video and update notes.
    • Beastmaster (August) (00:28:43): master/pet accuracy adjustments, a new jug pet, new moves for existing jug pets, and some shields' defense raised - extending the fight-alongside-your-pet concept and shoring up weaknesses. Master and pet gain an accuracy bonus when attacking the same enemy, and equipment properties will be revised in stages so gear can raise both master and pet accuracy together; a strong Subtle Blow effect will accompany this to prevent enemy TP gain doubling. The new jug pet has a never-before-seen appearance and deliberately spiky performance, adding fun in choosing pets situationally.
  • Skirmish adjustments (July) (00:32:19): Skirmish serves as the stepping-stone gearing content for fresh level 99s before high-end content, so it was adjusted to be more casual and playable. Details in the July update notes.
  • Odyssey adjustments (July) (00:39:26): the new, tougher area Sheol B was added. Trusts can be further strengthened there, and that strengthening also applies in Sheol A. Equipment upgraded with new Sheol B materials was previewed on video; entry conditions in the July update notes.
  • New scenario (August) (00:48:25): the first installment arrives in August. The story presumes completion of Rhapsodies of Vana'diel - if you haven't finished, aim to clear it by August, and veterans, please help anyone stuck on the final boss.

Further Ahead

  • New chat channel (00:54:59): a channel to bridge new/returning players and veterans is planned - newcomers can ask freely and veterans can help easily. Participation conditions (including standards for veterans) are being designed carefully so it stays true to its purpose.
  • Storage expansion (00:58:43): a paid storage expansion plan is planned for those who truly need it. Since more items unavoidably means longer load times, we're also considering not loading rarely used storage on area changes. This will take some time; details when there's progress.

Events and Campaigns (01:00:52)

  • July 2020 Login Campaign: headline item is the new Moogle Bed furnishing, with a "Moogle's cheer: experience up" aura.
  • Summer Umaumaya Campaign 2020: Skirmish-related campaigns timed with the Skirmish adjustments.
  • The Archduke Wants Attention Yet Again: new reward "Orchestrion Roll: Jeuno" - worth another run even if you got the equipment before.
  • Shadow Lord Day: a new one-handed sword reward is planned.
  • Trust Design Campaign (player vote): thanks for the many stat submissions; the dev team picked three finalists, and the top vote-getter will be implemented for a limited time - please vote!
  • Shield Design Contest: coming soon, with prizes for all participants.
  • Nomad Mog Bonanza: a new Bonanza variant planned for November and February - 200,000 gil per ticket, one ticket per character, and 3-digit numbers. (Not all future Bonanzas will use these rules.) Per Mocchi's on-air request, we'll do our best to prepare equipment with new graphics...!
  • Goods: hand towel (sold out) and the 18th anniversary T-shirt (XL still in stock at broadcast time).

Society is changing greatly, but Vana'diel remains its unchanging self. The next broadcast is planned for around September. See you then!
