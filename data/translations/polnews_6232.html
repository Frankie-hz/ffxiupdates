A notice for current PlayOnline users participating in the Xbox 360 FINAL FANTASY XI beta test.

The beta registration code can be linked to either a newly acquired PlayOnline ID or your existing PlayOnline ID. Please read the notes below and decide at your own discretion.

Worlds during the beta: the Xbox 360 beta runs on a dedicated beta world; during the beta you cannot play together with "PlayStation 2" and Windows players. After official service begins, users on all platforms will be able to play together on the same worlds, and a PlayOnline ID will be usable from any platform.

Characters: up to 16 characters total, including existing ones, can be created on the Xbox 360 version. Existing characters cannot be played on the beta world. Carrying beta character data into the official service is under consideration - details to be announced.

Notes: data may fail to carry over due to unexpected problems during the test; a content ID (character) once linked to a PlayOnline ID cannot be moved to a different ID; one PlayOnline ID cannot log in from multiple devices simultaneously.
