From the April 20 release of the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion data disc, severe congestion and processing delays occurred on the registration information management server, making registration extremely difficult for an extended period.

As emergency measures we reinforced the registration servers and adjusted congestion under 24-hour staffed monitoring, but the congestion was not resolved until Monday, April 24 of the following week. The registration servers had been considered, based on past experience, more than capable of handling the load, but the concentration of registrations greatly exceeded expectations.

We deeply regret failing to prevent this problem and sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused.

Registration is now processing normally, but if registrations were ever to concentrate at the same level or higher, the problem could recur. As a fundamental countermeasure we are planning a complete overhaul of the registration servers and programs, with thorough load testing at deployment, greatly increasing processing capacity so that even large-scale registration surges can be handled without trouble.

We apologize deeply to everyone who purchased Treasures of Aht Urhgan and to all players for the considerable inconvenience.

Koji Yamashita, General Manager, Online Operations Division, Square Enix Co., Ltd.
