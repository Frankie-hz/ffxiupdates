FINAL FANTASY XI server maintenance will be performed at the time below; the game is unavailable during maintenance.

Date/time: Tuesday, October 7, 2003, 16:00 to around 18:00 (JST; end time may change)

This maintenance carries out the migration to the Fairy world of characters that applied during the September 24 - October 1 application period and the October 4-6 additional period.

For migrating characters, some items cannot be carried over and are discarded at migration: items in the post and items in transit via the delivery service. Be sure to collect these before the maintenance. See also "World Migration Announcement (10/7)" under Important Notices.
