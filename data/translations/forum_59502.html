Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching the six-hour "Freshly Picked Vana'diel Special" for the 20th anniversary. The first half told FFXI's history through the testimony of many people involved; the second half covered the May and June version updates and fiscal 2022. Summary of the second half below.

20th Anniversary

  • 20th anniversary logo (03:46:14): designed by Ryoma Ito, chosen from many roughs. Asked why a chocobo: "the number 20 brought to mind getting my chocobo license - the memory of being the last in my linkshell able to ride one."
  • New Vana'diel march "We Are Vana'diel" (03:50:41): titled by composer Naoshi Mizuta, who sent a video message about the piece.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (May/June) (03:58:49): both revivals - Volume 1 is Mamool Ja (magic type), Volume 2 is Cetus. The fire and ice Mamool Ja's colors didn't match their elements, so colors and placement were changed. Cetus now takes damage more readily during its lightning and water modes - but striking the weakness also builds enmity quickly, so manage hate carefully. Meeting certain conditions now spawns up to three golden monsters; a balanced mix of magic and physical damage helps. June: Volume 1 is Qutrub (revival); Volume 2 in tuning. With the monster roster exhausted, new monsters are unlikely - future Ambuscades will be reworked, more playable versions of past ones, and from the April update the notes detail exactly what changed, so check them as you fight.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (May/June) (04:04:44): the eighth arc begins in May, starting with Maat and unfolding around the Grand Duchy of Jeuno - with a surprising someone making an appearance. Event-scene illustrations were previewed.
  • Mandragora★Mania (May) (04:07:18): the anniversary event from May 16 - rules explained briefly, plus the Moogle Shirt given at event start and new fireworks. The concurrent Adventurer Appreciation Campaign again offers the Aligned Shirt.
  • Master Levels: cap rises from 30 to 40 in June. On hunting grounds: Crawlers' Nest [S] worked because it happened to have unopened sections, but no such areas remain, and placing high-level monsters in existing areas tends to break quest progression - so further hunting areas are difficult.
  • Limited Trust revivals (04:14:04): Cornelia returns from May through the November update; Matsui-P returns from the November update. Trust questions were answered on air.
  • Thanks-to-All-Adventurers Campaign 2022 (04:21:23): the A.M.A.N. Trove special reward doubles from 5 to 10 million gil during the period. Also explained: the Sroda Belt bug fix (May update stops cure spells costing 0 MP; curative blue magic ignoring equipment penalties is intended), and Sroda Ring mechanics - its attack bonus applies at auto-attack and weapon skill execution so it won't show in /checkparam, and Trusts called by party members (not just your own) also count toward it. On rainbow chest rates from Mars vs. Venus Orbs: exact figures can't be shared - listen to NPC Greyson and judge; special reward odds are identical for both orbs.

20th Anniversary Bonanza, Campaigns, and Goods

  • 20th Anniversary Nomad Mog Bonanza (04:32:54): on sale through 23:59, June 15; exceptionally, two tickets per character this time. The new rank 3 Ocuba Crown (オクーバクラウン) was shown.
  • Welcome Back & Discount Campaigns: through ~16:59, May 31.
  • Goods (04:40:05): to mark the UT x FINAL FANTASY collab, the shield carried by Ark Angel EV is being distributed at login through 23:59 May 31. 20th anniversary goods on e-STORE preorder: Goblin plush, Mandragora plush, Shantotto plush, Mandragora mug, and a music box set (Ronfaure/Gustaberg/Sarutabaruta). Weekly Famitsu (May 19 issue) runs an FFXI feature; draft pages were previewed.

Further Ahead (04:54:24)

  • The Voracious Resurgence: just past its halfway point overall, with battlefields featuring fresher scenery in preparation.
  • Seasonal events: new rewards planned this year, starting with June's Sunshine Seeker.
  • Master Levels: cap to 40 in June, with a further raise around November; the final cap will be 50.
  • Odyssey: Vengeance and equipment rank cap raises planned - a 4th augment slot for weapons/armor and a 3rd for accessories.
  • New battle content: planned content using Castle Zvahl-style... rather, the outer courts of Ra'Kaznar, centered on party play, where unidentified accessories can be obtained - difficulty aimed at players finishing up Ambuscade equipment rather than endgame.
  • Empyrean armor reinforcement: +2/+3 materials will come from the new battle content. +2 should be achievable by small groups with some effort; +3, given the dramatic stat jump, will be a high hurdle to do alone. Both the new content and Empyrean +2 target a summer release.
  • Prime weapons: a new weapon line all players can build. No relic weapon required - progress in The Voracious Resurgence is the prerequisite. Why they bear that name and shape is told in the story itself.

Look forward to the next broadcast. See you then!
