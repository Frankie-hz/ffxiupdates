Rise of the Zilart Expansion Users Only

  • The new high-level region Tu'Lia opens; entering requires special conditions. It is also a Conquest region.
  • Artifact armor and quests for Samurai, Ninja, Dragoon, and Summoner are released.
  • Chocobo rental offices open in Kazham, Norg, and Rabao (the Norg office takes you through the Sea Serpent Grotto before mounting); Rabao and Kazham also gain chocobo shops. Chocobos can now be ridden in Ru'Aun Gardens (聖地ジ・タ), Yuhtunga Jungle, Yhoator Jungle, and East/West Altepa Desert.
  • Goblin Diggers now appear in the outlands. Outland airship fare drops to 200 gil; Nomad Moogle moving fees are abolished; outland bazaar taxes are abolished; Kazham's auction fee now matches the national formula.
  • Zilart-area beastmen (Tonberries, Sahagin, Antica, etc.) now drop gil and beastman coins.
  • Monster level and placement adjustments: lowered in Boyahda Tree, Gustav Tunnel, Ro'Maeve; raised in Den of Rancor, Ifrit's Cauldron, Labyrinth of Onzozo, Cape Teriggan; fewer worms in Kuftal Tunnel.
  • Fixed: in the Windurst mission "The Jester Who'd Be King" (王と道化師), kill credit was recorded for party members not on the mission. Fixed the outland quest 「画廊の迷宮」 quest monster failing to spawn. Alliance-capable battlefields added to quest battles. Protect IV and Protectra IV prices in Rabao lowered. Norg shells are no longer Exclusive and can be traded. Doors in the Sea Serpent Grotto that need the Sahagin key now admit others besides the key user.

All Users: Level Cap 70

  • The job level cap rises to 70; clear the quest "Riding on the Clouds." Combat and synthesis skill caps rise too - the current synthesis maximum is 90 ("Veteran").

Magic Adjustments

  • Cure III/IV/V MP costs adjusted; Cure II/III recast and Cure IV/V cast and recast times adjusted; all Cure spells now scale with healing magic skill. Cure V's enmity changed from cure-amount-based to a fixed value.
  • Erase's recast time shortened. Stoneskin now scales with MND and enhancing skill. Bar-element spells: shorter casting and duration, lower MP cost, effect now scales with enhancing skill.
  • Regen's price in Mhaura lowered; Regen II (WHM-only) now sold. Tier III/IV elemental MP costs lowered. Dispel is now resisted more by enemies with high dark resistance. Gravity's duration is now variable, 30-120 seconds. Poison-line duration now scales with enfeebling skill; Poison II and Poisonga II poison damage also scales with it.
  • Bard: high-level Etudes added, sold at Bastok's instrument shop (some scores require Bastok in 2nd place or better); Puppet's Operetta now sold by the Balleriano troupe; several other songs added. Magic Finale can now be resisted by enemies with high light resistance. Hunter's Prelude is now single-target.
  • Summoner: Carbuncle and Leviathan Blood Pacts Healing Ruby, Healing Ruby II, and Spring Water heal more, with lower MP cost on Healing Ruby II and Spring Water; avatar summoning MP costs lowered; Astral Flow effects raised overall; elemental spirit casting time shortened.
  • Ninjutsu: Ni-tier casting time greatly shortened (8 to 1.5 seconds). Monk: Chakra's reuse time cut from 15 to 5 minutes.
  • Newly added or unlocked: Shell IV (WHM68/RDM68), Shellra IV (WHM68), Regen (WHM21/RDM21), Regen II (WHM44), Regen III (WHM66), Protect IV (WHM63/RDM63/PLD70), Raise III (WHM70), Water III (BLM55/RDM67), Aero III (BLM59/RDM69), Thunder III (BLM66), Stone IV (BLM68), Water IV (BLM70), Aeroga III (BLM67), Firaga III (BLM69), Blizzard II (BLM42/RDM55/DRK66); songs: Knight's Minne IV (61), Enchanting Etude (62, CHR), Valor Minuet IV (63), Spirited Etude (64, MND), Learned Etude (66, INT), Foe Requiem VI (67), Quick Etude (68, AGI), Puppet's Operetta (69, silence resistance), Vivacious Etude (70, VIT).

Experience Changes

  • Experience at level 61+ has been returned to the same formula as level 60 and below: party level differences matter again, based on the highest-level member's relationship to the enemy plus the party size bonus. The experience table was revised to match. At 61+ it is easier to gain experience from stronger enemies; at 56-60 relative gains from stronger enemies dip slightly.
  • Leftover experience on level up now carries into the next level, including levels regained via Raise. A limiter now caps the KO experience penalty, easing the scaling loss at high levels. Dying in a level-restricted battle now costs experience based on the restricted level.

Cities and Convenience

  • Jeuno's population caps rise. Guards, auction agents, and delivery NPCs are added to every Jeuno level; bazaar tax becomes a uniform 10% (a junk-selling shop NPC is added to Ru'Lude Gardens).
  • Chocobo rental prices now fall 25% faster. Mog House exit selection menu changed (for expanded exits). Entering your Mog House no longer removes you from your party, and party flags can now be raised inside.
  • Delivery menu: items still in "preparing" status can be returned to your inventory individually with confirm/Enter (ordering may change); log messages now confirm taking out delivery items and receiving/discarding/returning post items. Door names in the target window are now uniformly green (with a few exceptions).
  • Party matching now extends region-wide. The "seek members" list initially shows only players near your level (widening it may cap at 100 results). Ordinary searches within a region/area also cap at 100; all-world searches now show 40 results (was 14). A "current region" search range was added: /search region or /sea region. Examples: /sea all 60 BLM; /sea region 35 DRG W.
  • Linkshell welcome messages can now be set, shown when members log in or first equip a pearl. /lsmes displays the message; /lsmes set (text), /lsmes clear manage it (permission-controlled); /lsmes level ls / ps / all sets who may overwrite it (owner only). The pearl creation button moved, and the linkshell menu no longer remembers cursor position.
  • New call variants: <ncall>/<ncallX> vibrates only players near the caller; <scall>/<scallX> plays sound only, no vibration. (Windows: vibration requires a force-feedback pad with vibration enabled.) /echo's message color can no longer be changed.
  • Fixed: items identical to one currently in your bazaar could not always be used via text command.

Synthesis and Items

  • Guild advanced-synthesis image support is now free, and its duration is 4x longer. Guild purchases now have a 3-second cooldown between buys. Desynthesis results now vary between several outcomes. Arrow and bolt prices lowered, with new arrow/bolt recipes in Woodworking and other crafts. Ninja tool synthesis yields greatly increased (with a few exceptions). Scavenge results changed. New items from logging.

Conquest

  • Expeditionary forces reworked: participants get a return-trip item ("魔行符") in advance and the old participation reward is gone; regional points come from defeating expedition monsters and opening chests, scaled heavily by ranking changes. Those who actually fought or opened chests are credited and receive rewards (only if the region is retaken; rewards vary with overall ranking shifts).
  • Unspent individual conquest points are no longer cleared when you change nations - they are preserved for your old nation (not transferable, but usable again if you return). Transfer fees adjusted. Beastman influence has grown in some regions.

Monsters and Battlefields

  • Beastman pet respawn changed from 30 to 60 seconds. Some summoned spirits now cast player-targeted cure/support magic outside battle. Your pet's damage messages now use the party-member chat color/filter category. Target lock now releases when battle ends. Shoal Pugils in Buburimu Peninsula no longer link. Goblin beastmaster placement changed in Batallia Downs, East Altepa Desert, and the Labyrinth of Onzozo.
  • Fixed rare attacks from monsters on other floors of Delkfutt's Tower. Fixed a bug in the pull-back handling when players leave a notorious monster's domain (Ash Dragon's domain is now the entire area).
  • New high-level notorious monsters added to both expansion and original areas, some with unusual spawn conditions or new appearances.
  • Beastmen's Seal orb rates changed: moon orb 99 to 60 seals, star orb 50 to 40. New comet orb (50 seals, level 50 cap battle). New Kindred's Seal (獣神印章) orbs for Qu'Bia Arena: Clotho, Lachesis, and Atropos orbs (30 Kindred's Seals each; contents differ by orb). The Clotho orb temporarily cannot be exchanged - pick another until it is fixed.
  • The level-40 Mandragora battle at the Balga's Dais Burning Circle now uses hand-to-hand type monsters and is a bit tougher.
  • Defeated monsters now show a different color on the compass. Fixed: THF/BST/RNG mains with NIN support were not getting the expanded compass (Wide Scan); NIN as main or support now widens compass detection.
  • A feature to re-watch past city mission events has been introduced.
  • Windurst quest 「正しいあいさつ」: the Cardian now appears at all times, not only under Windurst control. The item from Ranpi-Monpi at the Culinarians' Guild changed.
  • Each race's starting armor is now sold in the national shops (not tradeable and not bought back by NPC shops). Fixed unequippable items such as the grapnel appearing in equip menus.

Item Renames and Adjustments

  • Accessory names have been changed to match their effects (effects unchanged) - a long list of earrings and rings, e.g. カレッジピアス to 反射のピアス (Reflect Earring), スタミナピアス to 勇気のピアス (Courage Earring), and matching renames across the Vision/Knowledge/Victory/Solace/Chakra/Wealth/Trust/Passion/Spirit/Beauty/Empathy/Purity/Hades/Heavens earring lines and the corresponding ring lines, plus the Genius/Liberty/Grace/Wald/Wise/Travel/Energy earrings and Balance Ring (to 魅惑の指輪, Allure Ring).
  • 柔術用下ばき renamed 柔術下ばき. Equip level changes: Cipahlu (シパルー) 77 to 70; Highland Targe 76 to 70; Rogue's Culottes (盗賊の篭手 = Thief's Mufflers) 80 to 70; Heavy Cuirass 80 to 70; Pallas's Bracelet 70 to 60; Berserker's Axe 79 to 69; Colossal Lance 80 to 70; Nightmare Sword 75 to 70.
  • Following the golem/doll rename, Emet Pick help text changed from "vs. golems" to "vs. dolls."

Settings Backup

  • Macro and map marker settings can now be saved to PlayOnline, up to 4 characters (one saved character per "slot"). At character select: [L1+L3] or [Shift+Alt+Ctrl+B] to back up; [R1+R3] or [Shift+Alt+Ctrl+R] to restore; [R2+L2+R3+L3] or [Shift+Alt+Ctrl+D] to delete a saved file.
  • PS2 keyboard input: the triangle-button shortcut settings for normal/completion conversion have been reset - please reconfigure. Messages from the phrase-completion dictionary will in the future be auto-translated between the Japanese and English versions and displayed in the reader's language.
  • Windows version: full-width space input has been removed (the PS2 version never allowed it).
  • Known issues: consulate guards introduce themselves as "Jeuno Praetorian Guard" (they still function as national guards); a warning that individual conquest points "will be lost" may appear when transferring nations (they are in fact now preserved); Stun Claws shows equip level 68 but is equippable at 62; Rising Sun +1 shows THF/RNG but is actually THF/NIN.
