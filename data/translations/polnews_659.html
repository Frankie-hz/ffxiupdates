Yes... that season has come again this year! The moogles are busily preparing - "The full moon is soon, kupo! Let's work together to liven up the festival, kupo!" "Did you pack your bag full of mooncarrots, kupo? Handing them to lots and lots of adventurers is our job, kupo!"

With rabbit appearance points increased over last year, the moogles are hard at work. Adventurers: when you meet moogles at the many spots around the cities and beyond, listen well and receive a mooncarrot. Run across the vast lands of Vana'diel with the rabbits and enjoy a festival for one night only!

Event period: approximately 7 hours, from around 22:00 Friday, September 12 to around 5:00 Saturday, September 13, 2003 (JST).
