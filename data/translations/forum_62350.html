2024 Adventurer Survey - Interim Report (01:17:43)

The survey ran November 11-30; as of November 24 there were 12,271 responses (7,573 domestic, 4,698 overseas). Findings and answers:

  • Play status: about half of respondents in both regions actively play, and about 80% have played through Rhapsodies of Vana'diel. Login frequency suggests FFXI as a lifestyle. Play style split almost exactly half party / half solo - a result we consider important for development policy. Top update wishes: Japan leaned adjustments, overseas leaned additions.
  • "Trusts can't keep up at high content levels - make them stronger": given how many play solo, we intend to positively consider raising Trust capabilities.
  • "Add high-tier mission battlefields for Adoulin and Rhapsodies": planned for early 2025, using the battlefield previously seen only in Rhapsodies' final battle; among the rewards, the head piece shown uses the Cumulus Masque graphic and is planned as All Jobs.
  • Race change service progress: since FFXI has no item-shop billing, the plan is to purchase a period during which race change is enabled, then run the change event yourself; system discussions continue, so it will take a while longer. Face type and body size will also be changeable.
  • "Adjust existing content for IL119": a Limbus/Temenos renewal is in preparation, with the idea of pushing Artifact and Relic armor much further - plus mechanisms to skip intermediate reinforcement stages. Rather than Divergence-style instanced content, the aim is something like today's open Dynamis where anyone present can join.
  • Other comments - removing Divergence's player-count floor, Ambuscade armor +3, automaton attachment save/load - will be positively considered with the engineers.
  • Satisfaction: across all satisfaction levels, free comments cited "played out what's soloable" and "hard to play with others" - so reducing friction in playing together must guide us. Continuous login campaign satisfaction differed sharply between regions with "neither" dominating; monthly campaign satisfaction reflected the long repetition of the same campaigns - new ones like Vana'Bout have begun and more are in preparation.
  • Publishable survey results will be shared in some form; all feedback will inform future development. Thank you for the many responses.
