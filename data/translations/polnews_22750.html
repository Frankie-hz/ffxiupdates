Support arrangements over the year-end and New Year holidays:

  • Phone and chat support: closed Saturday, December 28, 2013 through Sunday, January 5, 2014.
  • Mail: answered in turn even during the closure, but replies may take longer than usual.
  • Game masters (FINAL FANTASY XI, FINAL FANTASY XIV, and Dragon Quest X in-game support): 24-hour support continues as normal; message replies may take time depending on the inquiry.
  • Post and parcels: held at the post office or carrier depot during the closure; delivered from Monday, January 6, 2014 onward.
