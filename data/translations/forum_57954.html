Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 54," covering the March and April version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (March/April) (00:08:12): March - Volume 1 is Tonberries (revival); Volume 2 is Leeches. Three leeches appear, each behaving distinctively; when one dies the others power up, so coordinate your kill timing. One uses Sand Breath whose power grows with its own remaining HP - beware. Depending on Trust composition, it should be somewhat easier than the earlier three-enemy Demon Ambuscade, making it good for repeat runs. April - Volume 1 is Meebles (revival); Volume 2 is in tuning, and given the short development window it may be an adjusted version of a past battle.
  • Odyssey (March) (00:12:22): boss monsters come to Sheol Gaol; fighting one requires having defeated all Atonement 1-3 monsters. New equipment was previewed on video (the BGM during this segment is a new piece by Naoshi Mizuta). The new equipment has four augment Types, chosen at first reinforcement; switching Types requires discarding the piece and repurchasing. Reinforcement uses RP earned by defeating (or damaging) the corresponding monsters at Vengeance +1 or higher, up to Rank 20; per-Type details were shown in the broadcast.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (March/April) (00:20:06): March continues the San d'Oria chapter. This arc is split into three parts rather than two for better story pacing - March is the middle part, April the finale.
  • Deeds (April) (00:22:02): reward items beyond 960 points will be added. As monthly content inevitably creates gaps based on when you started or returned, we can't keep adding powerful rewards like the Monberaux Trust; instead, post-960 rewards will be items also obtainable elsewhere that are pleasant to receive repeatedly, and the added tier will be resettable so the same rewards can be claimed again (only once Deeds accumulate enough to exchange for everything in the added tier). Contents are under consideration. Note: because of Golden Week, April's monthly resets (Records of Eminence, Hallmarks, etc.) come a little earlier than usual - watch your deadlines.

Early Spring Nomad Mog Bonanza 2021 (00:29:26)

We showed the rank 1 weapons' graphics and stats on video. The Adamas Aspis shield's "extends duration of abilities used on yourself" effect excludes SP abilities but does cover merit-point abilities. The true Brave Blade's "physical damage limit up with enmity" scales with both volatile enmity and cumulative (damage-based) enmity, so build both.
