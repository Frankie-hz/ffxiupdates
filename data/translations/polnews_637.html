A GM call function has been implemented on the PlayOnline Viewer as a means of handling inquiries about content other than FINAL FANTASY XI and about the Viewer itself.

Viewer GM calls let you contact a game master via text chat about problems with the Viewer or PlayOnline-compatible software, or nuisance behavior from other users. Beta hours: 11:00-19:00; requests outside these hours are not accepted.

Before making a GM call, check the Q&A first - it holds many questions and answers about FINAL FANTASY XI and PlayOnline.

GM calls cannot handle: opinions/requests for PlayOnline; bug reports for games; contract inquiries; problems occurring before PlayOnline login; or items already listed as known issues. Use the appropriate mail form or the Information Center phone line for those. For problems during FINAL FANTASY XI play, make the GM call in game where possible. Calls judged to fall into the above may be closed; if you believe yours was closed due to insufficient explanation, call again with clearer detail.
