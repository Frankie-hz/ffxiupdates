As the end of the month approaches, access concentrates and WebMoney period-extension procedures may become impossible to complete.

Customers who need to extend are shown guidance at PlayOnline login when an eligible paid content ID exists; follow the on-screen instructions to renew. The procedure can also be performed in the PlayOnline Viewer under Service & Support, "Member Information", "Payment Method", "WebMoney Extension".

Completing the procedure early avoids the access concentration and keeps everyone's procedures running smoothly. Customers whose content ID expires at month-end are shown the extension guidance; even after choosing "register later", the renewal can be done from Service & Support.
