Hello, this is Fujito. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 68," with guest voice actress Emiri Kato, centered on the May version update and changes to Vana'Bout. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (May-October) (00:16:10): all revivals - May: Volume 1 Tonberries, Volume 2 Cetus; June: Volume 1 Sahagin, Volume 2 Hecteyes. May's Cetus spawns the Golden Bomb up to three times - a good Hallmark opportunity. The monsters scheduled for July through October were also shown as a list in the video.
  • Master Trial (00:23:09): the new trial's opponents and battlefield were introduced. Participation now additionally requires clearing The Voracious Resurgence, and the battlefield's design sets participation at 3-6 players. The boss is stronger than any previous Master Trial - prepare thoroughly.
  • Vana'Bout changes (00:27:14): feedback-driven adjustments - dedicated Records of Eminence slots so your regular objectives aren't consumed; cleared objectives now swap automatically at the date change (uncleared ones remain attemptable until 23:59 of your next login day); objectives now extend beyond Ambuscade, with Plaudit gains scaled to difficulty; new items added to each prize tier and the Silver tier requirement lowered from 900 to 750 Plaudits. A ranking system debuts: opt-in, reflecting your Plaudits at registration, scoped to your own world, with anonymous entry possible; the top 10 receive an exclusive title after the event. The second Vana'Bout runs May 16 17:00 - May 30 16:59; all-world Plaudit totals viewable on the special site.

Further Ahead (00:50:24)

Early information for fiscal 2024 - some still at the consideration stage and not guaranteed:

  • High-level Besieged: long requested; we want the feeling of truly repelling an assault. The Serpent Generals will be adjusted too (enemy strength still being decided). Beastman strength starts at level 99-appropriate and rises in stages.
  • Ark Angel armor: Ark Angel armor adapted to all races is planned; distribution timing will be announced on the official site.
  • Same-account delivery icon: Ex items that CAN be delivered within an account currently require checking the forums; a dedicated icon in the item help window will make it visible in-game (shown image was development-stage).
  • Sortie Records of Eminence: objectives awarding a small coffer +1 are planned.
  • Dynamis Divergence entry timing: a system like the Rhapsody in Puce that banks "time since entry became available but unused" is under consideration.
  • PadConfig overhaul: functions previously unassignable to gamepads become assignable - target nearest NPC, open map, jump, and other frequent actions. The new UI was previewed.
  • Server replacement: continuing from last year - development environment servers are done; game servers are replaced this year.
  • Race change service: heavily requested from North America and being positively considered. Since FFXI has no item-shop system, the plan is to purchase a period during which race change is enabled, then trigger the change event yourself. Stats and race-specific equipment are affected, so it won't be something to use casually; paid vs. free is still being debated. Face type and body size will also be changeable.
