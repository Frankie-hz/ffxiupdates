The following latest versions have been added to the "additional dictionaries" that make it easy to convert words commonly used in PlayOnline-compatible software:

  • "PlayStation 2": FFXI comprehensive dictionary (July 10, 2007 version)
  • Windows: FFXI comprehensive dictionary (July 10, 2007 version)
  • Xbox 360: POL basic dictionary and FFXI comprehensive dictionary (July 10, 2007 versions)

The "PlayStation 2" and Xbox 360 dictionaries can be downloaded from "Additional Dictionary Download" in PlayOnline Topics and take effect when registered in the PlayOnline Viewer options. The Windows dictionaries can be downloaded from "Additional Dictionary for Windows" under Downloads on playonline.com and take effect when registered as Microsoft IME system dictionaries (not usable with IME 2007). More dictionaries will be published over time.
