The top page of FRONT MISSION ONLINE, the PlayOnline-compatible title releasing Thursday, May 12, 2005, has been renewed! The countdown to the imminent official service begins!

New pages include a "Start Guide" covering required environments and steps to begin play, and "A Day in the Life of a Soldier" introducing the flow of the actual game. Find it under Games in the main menu. The May 12 release is the "PlayStation 2" version.
