An update on the problem where automatic emails from the Square Enix Account Management System fail to arrive or are delayed: investigation confirmed that mail sent to specific addresses can be blocked. We are coordinating with the relevant parties toward a resolution, which is expected to take some more time; progress will be announced from Monday, March 10 onward. We apologize for the prolonged inconvenience.
