docomo, au, and SoftBank have each announced that carrier email cannot be used on their upcoming new rate plans.

If your Square Enix account's registered email address is a carrier email address and you are considering switching to one of the new plans, please change your registered email address beforehand (see the FAQ "How do I change my email address": https://sqex.to/Qz0AV).

With an unusable email address registered, you cannot change the registered address yourself, and the following Account Management System functions become unavailable:

  • Confirming your Square Enix ID and reissuing your password
  • Forced removal of a software token
  • Receiving automatic emails from the Account Management System

Resolving that situation requires contacting the Information Center and changing to a valid address after identity verification, which can take time depending on congestion. Because the registered address is an important function used in login trouble, please check your Square Enix account's registered email address periodically.
