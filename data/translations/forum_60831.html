Hello, this is Fujito. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 66," with guest voice actress Emiri Kato, centered on the second May version update. Summary below.

The Voracious Resurgence (00:12:30)

The final chapter arrived in the May 24 update. For those starting now, we compiled each arc's starting locations and NPCs. Some quests' dialogue changes depending on whether you've cleared the Summoner-related quests, so clear those too. The finale again centers on the Destiny Destroyers, with new music implemented for the occasion. A post-story epilogue in the style of the Chains of Promathia aftermath quests is planned for a future update. Also: clearing the story raises the Gallimaufry accumulation cap from 100,000 to 100 million - you'll need plenty for prime weapon reinforcement, so run Sortie at your own pace.

Prime Weapons (00:32:01)

After clearing The Voracious Resurgence, prime weapons can be reinforced to their final stage. The materials needed from stage 3 onward were introduced (also listed in the update notes). Like the other ultimate weapon lines, primes aren't finished quickly - temper them steadily. The broadcast also showed the final-stage weapon list and the effect of the instrument-exclusive song "Aria of Passion" (冷静と情熱のアリア) - see the video.

Ambuscade (May/June) (00:53:37)

Both months are revivals: May - Volume 1 Orcs, Volume 2 Flies; June - Volume 1 Quadav, Volume 2 Uragnites. No battle adjustments - dust off your old tactics. May's Flies bring the Golden Bomb for the anniversary month; a good chance to farm Hallmarks.

FFXI This Fiscal Year (00:55:58)

Ambuscade continues its regular rotation, but with past monsters returning rather than newly adjusted ones. Several new Master Trials are planned as proving grounds for reinforced prime weapons. Battlefields with story elements and cross-world cooperative goal events are also under consideration.

Announcements (01:03:15)

  • Welcome Back & Discount: Welcome Back has ended; Discount runs through May 31 17:00.
  • May Login Campaign: the headline item "Eeh-jis" (えぇぢす) synergizes into the "Eee-jis" (いぃぢす) - the broadcast revisits the April Fools' joke it came from.
  • Spring Trust Campaign: May 1-31.
  • Adventurer Appreciation Campaign: May 16 - July 3; Mandragora★Mania runs through May 31.
  • 21st Anniversary Nomad Mog Bonanza: on sale from May 17, two per character; rank 3 features recolors of Salvage equipment.
  • A.M.A.N. Trove Campaign: two silver Mutual Aid vouchers at first daily login, from May 18.

Dissidia Final Fantasy Opera Omnia (01:22:33)

The story released April 28 introduces Selh'teus (CV: Koki Uchiyama); story and battle footage were shown. Lilisette (Emiri Kato), Lion, Prishe, and the other FFXI heroines are active there too - official site.

New Goods and Exhibition (01:27:33)

Several in-production goods were previewed - T-shirts and some surprising items under consideration; prices and dates to come. And in August 2023, an offline exhibition of unpublished materials, "The Hidden Treasures of Vana'diel," will be held - details on the official site and official Twitter.

Look forward to the next broadcast. See you then!
