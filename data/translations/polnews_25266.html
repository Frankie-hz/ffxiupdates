From around 10:00 (JST) on Tuesday, June 26, 2018, TLS 1.0 communication will no longer be permitted for the following payment methods used with Square Enix accounts, as a security improvement. Only TLS 1.1 and TLS 1.2 will be permitted, so depending on your browser settings these payment methods may become unusable.

Affected payment methods: au Kantan Kessai; Docomo payment.

If you use these payment methods, please check your browser settings and enable TLS 1.1/1.2. In summary: on Windows, update Internet Explorer 11, Firefox, or Chrome to the latest version (for IE 10 and earlier, enable "Use TLS 1.1" and "Use TLS 1.2" under Internet Options, Advanced); on Mac OS X, update Safari via Software Update or update Chrome; on smartphones, update iOS or the Android system software; on game consoles, update the system software.

Update (June 5, 2018): the cutoff date was changed from June 6 to June 26, 2018.
