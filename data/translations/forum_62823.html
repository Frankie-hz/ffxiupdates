Direction for Year 23 (00:52:54)

  • Updates centered on "enriching individual play": "individual" doesn't mean solo play - it means each player. The goal is that you can log in, access what you want to do, play it, and log out without your personal activity being blocked. FFXI was originally designed around group play, and the survey showed both that people can't devote that time today and that forming groups itself - and aligning on tactics afterward - can feel like a burden; this is now a principle FFXI will honor.
  • Content expansion: continuing last year's pattern - high-level Besieged, the Cloud of Darkness battlefield "★Gleam of the Magatama" (勾玉の輝き), and new battlefields like "Rondo in Pink" - with the New Limbus as this year's installment.
  • More item acquisition opportunities: the aim is to remove stress from obtaining reinforcement materials and story-progression items - reducing wasted time like hundreds of kills with no drop - via additional acquisition routes and drop rate adjustments, fitting modern play styles.
  • Trust strengthening: a Trust called by a player in item-level gear is effectively close to a party member, and many players gauge content by that strength - but while players keep getting stronger through new IL119 gear, Trusts have had no way to keep up. We are planning to raise Trusts further to widen everyone's range of activity; specifics are being planned within this year's schedule.

Campaigns and Events

  • Welcome Back & Discount (01:01:57): returnees, see the video guides - part 1 (install/config) and part 2 (gamepad/in-game settings).
  • Seasonal events: reward additions under consideration, and start/end times are being standardized to 17:00 start / 23:59 end for clarity.
  • Login campaigns (01:05:52): in line with the Trust policy, some hard-to-get Trusts now appear at 10 points, with the lineup rotating monthly so a fair Trust collection can be built from login campaigns alone. This month's highlight: Prishe's Boots for the anniversary - instead of the usual two-copies synergy for HQ, you follow letters from Prishe around the world to gather what's needed. More NPC-themed equipment like this is planned - including pieces with real stats, not just vanity.
  • 23rd Anniversary Thanks Campaign (01:13:10): themed around making IL119 equipment easier to build, tied to the year's plans. Monthly campaigns will also see adjustments, changes, and new additions going forward.
  • Vana'Bout (01:14:18): May adds objectives for nostalgic quests - Windurst's fish check, Bastok's "The Siren's Tear," and other early-adventure quests. The Ferry objective adds the quus (キュス) as a target fish - hard to catch with high fishing skill, and yes, that's deliberate: it's a game, so "clear the game." Points you earn benefit everyone. 150% achievement adds a limited-time way to obtain the Moogle Masque - the item from VanaFest 2012 - planned to be acquired using seasonal event items. The special site is being renewed for readability.
  • 23rd Anniversary Nomad Mog Bonanza (01:26:21): rank 3 adds artisan torques; rank 2 adds Crepuscular equipment (spoils of the ★Shinryu high-tier battlefield); rank 1 adds "Mog's magic crystal," the Bonanza weapon reforge material. The sale window is shorter this time (through the June update) as an experiment toward holding Bonanzas more often.

May Version Update

  • Besieged (01:29:46): the Morale gauge is in - raising it grants buffs according to your Serpent General quest completion. The Generals now use job-appropriate abilities (Rughadjeen uses Sentinel, Gadalar uses Mana Wall). March level 11 added, at roughly content level 109, with further march/content level raises planned.
  • Weapon skill quest relief (01:35:01): latent-breaking modernized - points per weapon skill use and per skillchain increased, finishing in about a fifth of the previous time.
  • Artifact/Relic cost reduction (01:36:32): materials needed to take IL119 Artifact/Relic armor to +2/+3 reduced (figures in the update notes) - groundwork so players can reach the June New Limbus sooner.
  • Race change service (01:38:24): live since May 19. Its focus is not casual shapeshifting but letting players who picked the wrong race long ago enjoy Vana'diel as they wish - hence a paid service, partly as a deliberate threshold. Race/gender-exclusive equipment stays in your possession but can't be worn if requirements aren't met; Dancer's exclusive attire, given its difficulty, can be exchanged. Hairstyle-only or size-only changes are possible too. Details on the special site.

June

  • Adventurer Appreciation Campaign: June 2 17:00 - July 9 23:59; weekly logins grant items, with the Vana'clock furnishing at the fourth login.
  • Shami'n Trove: back by popular demand with the bug fixed, June 2-9, again using Pandemonium (ポボス) orbs; occasional reruns planned.
  • New Limbus (June update) (01:49:09): Limbus renewed for high levels - no area occupancy, party or solo, and a new avenue for Artifact/Relic reinforcement. Entry requires the White/Black Cards (acquisition unchanged; once obtained, never needed again) plus the "Rhapsody in Fuchsia" (星唄の煌めき【終奏】) from clearing Rhapsodies of Vana'diel. No time limit inside, unlimited re-entry, Trusts capped at 3 per party. You choose a level at entry and monsters attack at that level - the higher the level, the more reinforcement points earned. June ships regular monsters only; boss-class NMs come in later updates. Records of Eminence based on old Limbus become uncompletable - abandon any you hold. Reinforcement uses separate point types from Temenos and Apollyon (pick your destination by the gear you want), traded at the in-content smelting forge - looks like a synergy furnace, but no synergy skill or controls needed.
  • Seasonal: Sunshine Seeker runs, and Vana'Bout 150% adds the Moogle Masque opportunity.
  • Mog Bonanza Weapon giveaway: announced on air - a coupon exchangeable for a Mog Bonanza Weapon for everyone who logs in by 23:59 June 9 (exchange itself has no deadline). Post-reinforcement stats published here for reference.
