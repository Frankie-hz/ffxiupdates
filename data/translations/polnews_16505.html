We have received many inquiries about the Square Enix Security Token, whose sales resumed Monday, June 29. The most frequent topics:

Setting up the Square Enix account and security token: when your token arrives, confirm the setup procedure at http://www.playonline.com/cis/jp/about.html

Cautions about deregistering a Square Enix account:

  • A deregistered Square Enix account cannot be restored.
  • If you deregister a Square Enix account after linking it to a PlayOnline ID, the PlayOnline ID also cannot be restored.
  • If you may resume using PlayOnline, do not deregister the Square Enix account - instead cancel the content IDs in the PlayOnline Viewer. If you have deregistered the account by mistake, contact our Information Center.
