A notice for customers using the software token.

If you change the smartphone on which your software token is registered, or delete the token app, you must perform a "forced removal" and then re-register the software token. Before changing phones or deleting the app, be sure to note down your emergency removal password. If forced removal cannot be performed, you will be unable to log in to services using that Square Enix account.

Checking the emergency removal password: log in to the Square Enix Account Management System; the password is shown under "Token Usage Status" on the right of the screen after login. The password changes each time it is used, so note the new one after each use.

Forced removal and re-registration: on the forced removal page, enter your Square Enix ID, password, and emergency removal password; a removal URL is sent to your registered email address, valid for 60 minutes. To re-register, log in to the Account Management System, choose Services/Options, then One-Time Password, then software token registration, install the app for your device (iPhone/iPod touch or Android), have the registration password mailed to you, and enter your Square Enix ID, registered birth date, and the registration password in the app within 60 minutes.

If you cannot perform forced removal yourself (for example, you have forgotten the emergency removal password), the Information Center can remove it after identity verification.
