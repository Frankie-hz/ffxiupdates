Server maintenance accompanying a FINAL FANTASY XI version update will be performed at the following time. The client program also updates; after maintenance, pressing the Play button starts the update automatically.

Date/time: Thursday, October 23, 2003, 9:00 to around 11:00 (JST)
Affected: FINAL FANTASY XI players

Main changes:

  • Made various changes in preparation for the official North American service launch.
  • Fixed an issue where "shoes" could no longer be crafted.
  • Corrected the status display of the Bomb Queen Ring.
  • Fixed an issue where a right mouse click could fail to cancel during text input.

The following issues introduced by the October 21 version update have been confirmed and are being investigated:

  • The help message of the "food order slip" item from a San d'Oria quest does not display correctly.
  • Some attacks used by charmed monsters and avatar attacks can damage the pet itself.
  • At the auction house, the text color of Sleepga and Sleepga II does not correctly reflect usability. The correct jobs/levels are: Sleepga - BLM Lv.31; Sleepga II - BLM Lv.56. Already-learned copies also fail to show the learned color.
  • Some combinations of character face and head equipment do not display correctly.
