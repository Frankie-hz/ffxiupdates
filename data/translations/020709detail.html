Job Adjustments

  • Warrior: Hand-to-Hand combat skill rating lowered by one grade.
  • Monk: Off-hand attack accuracy changed. The effects of the job abilities Hundred Fists and Chakra have been changed. Parameters on monk equipment have been changed, and a new attack motion has been added.
  • White Mage: The job ability Benediction now generates less enmity.
  • Red Mage: The black magic spell Gravity is now obtainable. The skill caps for Dagger, Sword, and Club, as well as maximum MP, have been raised.
  • Thief: The skill caps for Hand-to-Hand and Sword have been raised. The job ability Steal now generates less enmity.
  • Dark Knight: Great Sword weapon skills can now be used.
  • Bard: The song Horde Lullaby (puts enemies in an area to sleep) is now obtainable. The duration of bard songs has been shortened across the board, so songs must be re-sung more frequently than before. The skill caps for Dagger, Sword, and Club have been raised, and the shop prices of scores have been lowered.
  • Ranger: The ranger-only weapon type "gun" is now available. Along with this, the shop prices of arrows and other ranged-attack consumables have been lowered, and the stack limit for arrows and bullets has been changed from 12 (one dozen) to 99. Arrows and bullets created via synthesis also stack to a maximum of 99, but auction house listings must likewise be in units of 99. Several other ranger-only items have been added besides guns. The skill caps for Dagger and Sword have been raised.
  • Beastmaster: The duration of a monster charmed with the job ability Charm is now fixed at 5 minutes, and the extension from Familiar is fixed at 30 minutes. However, in proportion to the pet's remaining HP, there is a risk that charm will break when the pet takes a critical hit, regardless of remaining time. Keep an eye on your pet's HP.

Battle Adjustments

  • Dagger weapon skills are now restricted for jobs other than Red Mage, Ranger, Thief, and Bard.
  • The accuracy correction for level difference introduced in the July 2, 2002 version update has been relaxed and switched to a damage correction. Monsters intended for levels 70 through 80 are already placed in Vana'diel, set to become defeatable as the level cap is raised in the future. In line with this change, the enemy strength check messages from the Check command have been further subdivided; opponents with a level gap too large for reasonable combat are now shown as "incredibly tough" and similar. Take care not to approach such opponents carelessly, and look for dungeons and fields suited to your own level.
  • In connection with the above, the problem where solo play had become more time-efficient for gaining experience than party play has been addressed by revising party battle bonuses again. Within the appropriate range described above, fighting tough enemies is more advantageous in a party.
  • As previously announced, the drop rates for monsters in some dungeons (Ghoul, etc.) have been increased. Monster drops and placement throughout dungeons in general have also been changed. These changes are scheduled to continue next week on Tuesday, July 16.

System and Interface

  • Config menu > Windows: eight window patterns can now be selected.
  • Config menu > Misc.: overall screen brightness correction is now available. (PlayStation 2 version only; the Windows version keeps per-RGB gamma correction as before.)
  • Moving while healing now automatically cancels healing.
  • The target window now also displays the target's name color and icons.
  • The text commands /attack on, /attack off, and /equip have been added. See the text command list for details.
  • The text commands /names off and /names on have been added, useful for taking screenshots without character names. When using screenshots and other PlayOnline copyrighted material on personally run fan sites, please follow the "Guidelines for the Use of PlayOnline Copyrighted Material"; all other use is strictly prohibited.
  • The map screen now shows the coordinate block of your current position.
  • To reduce players overlapping right after an area change, slight position correction is now performed.
  • New motions have been set for the following existing emote commands: /kneel, /sigh, /comfort, /huh, /blush, /angry, /disgusted, /upset, /panic, /fume, /doubt, /sulk, /shocked.

Quests, Synthesis, and Economy

  • Various new quests have been added in many places. Rewards for some existing quests have also been changed.
  • Various new synthesis recipes have been added at each guild. As noted above, one synthesis of arrows or bullets now yields up to 99.
  • The fishing hook check has been changed; the chance of the catch escaping after a hit has been reduced.
  • Auction house listing slots have been reduced from 8 to 7 per player. In exchange, bidding can now be done freely regardless of how many items you have listed.
  • Explanation NPCs have been placed in front of each auction house. Please review how the auction house system works.
  • When checking the post in your Mog House, moving the cursor over gil sent from the auction house now shows in the log window which listed item it was payment for.
  • The numeric input interface in the price setting window, level search window, and similar has been made easier to use.
  • Item detail descriptions now appear when trading individual conquest points to Conquest Guards for items.
