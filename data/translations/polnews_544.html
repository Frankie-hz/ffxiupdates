Article 26 of the PlayOnline Member Agreement has been amended as follows. Per Articles 4.2 and 4.3, the revised agreement takes effect August 15, 2003.

Added provisions - Article 26, Prohibited member conduct:

  • (19) Using any PlayOnline content or service (including the community features) by means of a program or tool that automatically repeats specific actions.
  • (20) Using any PlayOnline content or service (including the community features) in a manner that automatically repeats specific actions using any kind of device.

The former items (19) onward of Article 26 have been renumbered from (21) onward, with no change to their content.

The "Rules & Manners" section and FINAL FANTASY XI "Online Manners" have also been updated accordingly. Rules & Manners can be found under PlayOnline Service & Support, and Online Manners under Rules & Manners on the FINAL FANTASY XI top page.
