For customers paying for PlayOnline services with a DC Card, Bank Card, or Aeon Card, PlayOnline paid-content fees may not appear on your card statement. This is an effect of the card companies' procedures, and the charges will be settled together in the following month or later. Affected customers will not have their PlayOnline service suspended because of this.

For statement details, contact your card company; for PlayOnline contract details, contact our Information Center.
