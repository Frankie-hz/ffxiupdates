Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 52." The broadcast covered the September and October version updates - and since October is a quieter month ahead of November (the month of XI), some information from beyond that too. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • New chat channel: Assist Channel (November) (00:07:07): planned for the November update, with the client UI shipping early in October. With the new scenario and more time spent at home, new and returning players are increasing; this gives them an in-game place to consult senior adventurers. Social media works for some, but a casual in-game consultation channel matters for FFXI's future. Channel rules will be detailed at implementation.
  • Ambuscade (September/October) (00:14:01): September - Volume 1 is Quadav (revival), Volume 2 is Matamata. The Matamata has two modes: very tough in normal mode, but a certain special move triggers it to start spinning - its attacks get trickier, but damage lands more easily. October - Volume 1 is Trolls (revival); Volume 2 still being tuned.
  • The Voracious Resurgence, part 2 (September) (00:18:21): the second half of the previous story arrives. We again showed the concept illustration by Akihiro Yamada (Record of Lodoss War, The Twelve Kingdoms); the goblin and watermelon-mandragora (Citrullus) from the art appear in this chapter. Quest rewards come at each story milestone. Going forward, roughly 3-month arcs with first/second halves, then about a month of production before the next arc - so October is a production month and a new arc begins in November. If a story grows in the writing, arcs may become three parts or get longer production gaps.
  • Odyssey (September) (00:34:59): the new area Sheol C added, CL roughly 129+ (entry conditions in the September update notes). We previewed several of the newly reinforceable Wanted equipment pieces. The gains may look modest next to the previous stage, but the properties raise broadly useful parameters - try them in practice. FFXI's equipment philosophy is to never make simple strict upgrades, and within that we'll keep making equipment you actually want. This completes the Wanted-reforge purpose of Odyssey, but a new form of Odyssey update is under consideration that should make your accumulated upgrades matter.
  • Bug fix (September) (00:46:08): some monsters reduce damage when hit repeatedly by a particular attack type. This was also applying to the second and later hits of multi-hit blood pact moves - unintended, now fixed.
  • Dark Matter / Arcane Glypt campaign change (September): previously, talking to the NPC during the NQ period on switchover day meant no extra HQ glypt uses after the switch; now the HQ allotment is credited at switchover even in that case.

Tentative November Preview ("Provisionally Provisional Kupo")

  • Assist Channel details (00:53:01): no participant cap (unlike Unity chat); speaking requires meeting certain conditions. As a new experiment, a mute-list-like feature is planned - distinct from the blacklist, operated by players holding that privilege. Harassment is properly a GM matter, but the Assist Channel is highly visible and needs faster response, so a player-managed mute list should help prevent trouble.
  • The Voracious Resurgence, chapter 2: text checking is in full swing - no spoilers, please look forward to it.
  • Nomad Mog Bonanza: as previously announced - 200,000 gil per ticket, one per character, 3-digit numbers only.

Contests

  • Trust Design Campaign results (01:01:30): thanks for all the entries and votes - results on the topics page. All finalists were unique and genuinely viable designs.
  • Shield Design Contest: entries closed with many submissions; tallying and judging under way. Some entries were shown on the broadcast.
