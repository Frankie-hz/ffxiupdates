Around 18:00 on Tuesday, June 6, 2006, Microsoft performed an Xbox 360 system update. After applying it, an issue was confirmed in the Xbox 360 PlayOnline Viewer and FINAL FANTASY XI where some characters cannot be typed on the keyboard. A fix is in progress and expected within a day or two; the software keyboard can be used to enter the affected characters meanwhile.

Confirmed issues: the "@" key toggles full/half-width (Viewer only); in kana input, the "¥" key enters "ro" and the "\" key enters "-"; "\" cannot be used as a key assignment (FFXI only); PrintScreen does not take screenshots (FFXI only).

These problems were fixed by the Xbox 360 client program version update performed at 2:00 on Wednesday, June 7, 2006.
