Support arrangements over the year-end and New Year holidays:

  • Phone and chat support: closed Thursday, December 30, 2010 through Monday, January 3, 2011.
  • Mail: mail received during the closure will be answered in turn from Tuesday, January 4, and replies may take longer than usual.
  • Game masters (in-game support): 24-hour support continues as normal. Depending on the content of a GM call, replies are given by message and may take time.
  • Post and parcels: held at the post office or carrier depot during the closure; delivered from January 4 onward.
