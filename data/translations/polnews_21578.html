In the emergency maintenance performed 2:00-4:00 on Thursday, June 28, 2012, the bugs where Bonanza Coupon S could disappear from the Festive Moogle's choices, and where it could be received repeatedly, were fixed.

Receiving method change: previously, up to 8 items from registered campaign codes were shown as choices to pick freely. Now, items are shown one at a time in code-registration order; receive the shown item, then speak to the Festive Moogle again for the next.

Customers who registered multiple Bonanza Coupon S codes before the change: only one item per character can be received from the Festive Moogle; the second and subsequent coupons will be sent separately by delivery. Codes registered after the change can all be received from the Festive Moogle.

Deadline extension: Bonanza Coupon S codes (only) now expire 23:59, Monday, July 2, 2012. Other codes keep their original deadlines; VanaFest 2012 Random Deal codes expire 23:59, Saturday, June 30, 2012.

The NPC "Dealer Moogle" returned at the end of the emergency maintenance.
