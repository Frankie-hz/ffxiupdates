Congratulations on repelling the giant creature! It was defeated on every world.

On worlds that succeeded, rewards from each government will be placed for a limited time in San d'Oria, Windurst, and Bastok in the hope they aid your adventures. Search every corner of the cities!

During the event we received many GM calls reporting that the monster could not be seen. Explanation: the target monster "GiganToad" could not move of its own accord while in combat, and with players concentrated so heavily, its target switching only moved to nearby players, so it stayed in nearly one spot. Player numbers then far exceeded the display limits of the client machines, and the display clipping function (which culls distant characters when VRAM capacity is exceeded) made the monster invisible unless you were very close. As a result most players near the monster could not see or attack it. We apologize that some players who traveled to the site could not join the battle.

We will take these results into account so similar problems do not occur in future events. Look forward to them!
