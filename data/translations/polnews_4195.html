In line with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information taking effect April 1, 2005, Article 12.1 of the PlayOnline Member Agreement and the personal information contact point have been updated and added as follows. The revised agreement applies from April 1, 2005.

Updated - Article 12, Management of personal information: the company will not use members' personal information for any purpose other than: (1) customer support; (2) informing members by mail or post about products, services, events, and surveys of the company, its affiliates, and partner companies; (3) billing and payment collection for paid content; (4) purposes separately set out in the agreement. Handling of personal information otherwise follows the separately published "Guidelines on the Handling of Personal Information."

Added: a contact point for personal information inquiries (disclosure, correction, deletion, etc.) has been appended to the agreement: privacypolicy@square-enix.co.jp

The "Rules & Manners" section has also been partially updated; it can be viewed from PlayOnline Service & Support.
