The FFXI comprehensive dictionary (March 10, 2008 version) has been added to the "additional dictionaries" that make it easy to convert words commonly used in PlayOnline-compatible software.

The "PlayStation 2" and Xbox 360 dictionaries are downloadable from "Additional Dictionary Download" in PlayOnline Topics (register via the Viewer options); the Windows dictionaries from "Additional Dictionary for Windows" under Downloads on playonline.com (register as a Microsoft IME system dictionary). More dictionaries will be published over time.
