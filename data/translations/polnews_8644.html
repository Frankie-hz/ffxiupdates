Verification of the driver software versions below for graphics cards confirmed to work with FINAL FANTASY XI for Windows has been completed by our development staff in cooperation with the manufacturers. Download drivers from each maker's website.

Verified graphics drivers (as of September 26, 2006):

  • NVIDIA - FORCEWARE 91.47 (requires DirectX 9.0c): GeForce 7 series (excluding 7950, 7100, GX2; PCI-Express only). FORCEWARE 66.93: GeForce 6 series (excluding cards unsupported by 66.93), GeForce 4 Ti, GeForce 4 MX, GeForce 3, GeForce 2 MX (32MB+ VRAM). FORCEWARE 56.64: GeForce FX series (verified with BumpMap disabled in FINAL FANTASY XI Config - open the 3D Map tab, uncheck BumpMapEnable, close and save; the config cannot be changed while playing).
  • ATI Technologies - Catalyst 5.13: RADEON X1800/X1600/X1300/X850/X800/X700/X600/X300 and RADEON 9800/9700/9600/9500/9200/9000 series.

Notes: verification OS is Windows 2000/XP; other products are still being verified. Mobile chips (Mobility RADEON 9000, GeForce FX 5600 Go, nForce2 IGP, etc.) were verified only as complete PC models jointly with the PC makers - the chips alone are not guaranteed. Some part combinations may not work; obtain and apply drivers at your own responsibility. Results are as of September 2006; newer drivers and some cards may not be recognized. For maker-built PCs, use the graphics drivers provided by the PC maker.
