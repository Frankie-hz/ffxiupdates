A roundup of the official social media guides for returning players, organized by topic (each item links to an official video or X post):

  • Set up your play environment: download/install walkthrough, screen resolution settings, and customizing the on-screen display (YouTube videos).
  • Collect Trusts (Alter Egos): the step-by-step path to unlocking the Records of Eminence objective that yields the key item raising Trust levels - tutorial-quest Trusts (Excenmille, Naji, Kupipi), RoE Trusts (Valaineral, Mihli Aliapoh, Tenzen, Adelheid, Joachim), mission 2-3 Trusts (Kuyin Hathdenna... Curilla, Ayame, Nanaa Mihgo), mission 5-2 Trusts (Trion, Volker, Ajido-Marujido), and Shantotto via her related quest. Also covers the Echoes of Vana'diel crossover Trusts (Prishe, Zeid, Lilisette, Shantotto).
  • Raise your item level: the recommended gearing ladder - IL117 gear from Records of Eminence, IL119 from Peacekeepers' Works (Sparks), IL119 from Ambuscade, upgrading Ambuscade gear, and IL119 from Domain Invasion.
  • Upgrade job attire (Artifact): acquiring artifact armor and its multi-stage upgrades to +2, +3, and +4.
  • Obtain mounts: guides for the specially-acquired Omega and Ixion... (Levitus) mounts.
  • Other tips: using the item search command to find where you stored something.
