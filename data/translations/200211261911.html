Level Cap Raised to 60

  • The level cap has been raised to 60, unlocking items and magic for levels 56-60. To pass level 56 you must clear the quest "Atop the Highest Mountains" (no items required).
  • Spells now usable (with learning levels; 白=WHM, 赤=RDM, 黒=BLM, 暗=DRK, ナ=PLD, 吟=BRD): Shell III (WHM57/RDM57/PLD60), Protectra III (WHM47), Shellra II (WHM37), Shellra III (WHM57), Raise II (WHM56), Reraise II (WHM60), Barvirus (RDM39), Barvira (WHM39), Barpetrify (RDM43), Barpetra (WHM43), Sleep II (BLM41/RDM46/DRK56), Tractor (BLM25/DRK32), Aero III (BLM59/RDM76/DRK91), Stone III (BLM51/RDM66/DRK79), Water III (BLM55/RDM71/DRK85), Firaga II (BLM53), Blizzaga II (BLM57), Aeroga II (BLM48), Stonega II (BLM40), Waterga II (BLM44), Flare (BLM60), Freeze (BLM50), Tornado (BLM52), Quake (BLM54), Burst (BLM56), Flood (BLM58), Ice Spikes (BLM20/RDM40).
  • New bard songs: Sword Madrigal (51), Dragonfoe Mambo (53), Gold Capriccio (54), Mage's Ballad II (55), Shining Fantasia (光明のファンタジア) (56), Foe Requiem V (57), Battlefield Elegy (59), Victory March (60), Foe Lullaby (single target) (16).
  • Other magic changes: Raise usable by RDM at 38; Viruna by WHM at 34; Holy MP cost 75 to 100, range 25m to about 15m, accuracy now depends on MND, casting slightly longer; Flare-class attack magic accuracy now depends on INT; Silence usable by RDM at 18; Invisible RDM25; Sneak RDM20; Deodorize RDM15; Sleep DRK30; Bind RDM11/DRK20; Cure now weighted more by caster MND than target VIT; MP costs of tier II black/elemental damage spells revised.

Artifact Armor

  • Job-specific artifact equipment and the artifact quests needed to obtain it have been added. November introduces the six basic jobs; extra jobs will follow.
  • Equipment changes: new looks for Philosopher's Circlet (哲人のサークレット), Volunteer's Slops (義勇兵の下衣), Brigandine, Brigandine +1; Demonic Sword added-effect message fixed; Earth Wand added-effect element fixed (fire to earth); some instruments now boost specific songs.
  • Improved stats or expanded equippable jobs for: Terra's Leggings +1 (大地の脚絆+1), Chain Hose +1, Lizard Belt +1, Lizard Belt, Volunteer's Baghnakhs (義勇兵の爪), Bone Axe +1, Mythril Axe +1, Kaman +1, Maple Wand +1, Brass Rod +1, Great Bow +1, Battle Bow +1, War Bow +1, Crossbow +1, Zamburak +1, Arbalest +1, Heavy Crossbow +1, Brigandine +1, Opal Ring.

Areas and Monsters

  • The following areas now target higher levels with heavily revised monster placement: Eldieme Necropolis, Crawlers' Nest, Fort Ghelsba (ガルレージュ要塞 = Garlaige Citadel), Castle Zvahl Baileys. Monster spawn points for the quest "In Defiant Challenge" moved accordingly but now appear in more locations.
  • Minor placement adjustments (recommended level largely unchanged): Fei'Yin, Xarcabard, Castle Zvahl Keep.
  • The Cursed Statue curse effect in Beadeaux has been shortened.
  • The magicked gates in the Toraimarai Canal can now also be opened at any time with the key item "magicked seal" (魔封門の札) obtained through a certain quest, in addition to the old method.
  • The requirement for the Crawlers' Nest map has been slightly relaxed.
  • Treasure Coffers, a higher tier of chest, now appear in some dungeons. Depending on the relative value of a monster level band's drops, some items may drop several at once.

Monster Targeting (Enmity) Overhaul

  • Monster targeting logic has changed. Previously each player action was evaluated individually over a short span; now accumulated damage taken is weighted much more heavily, and evaluation is multi-dimensional. Monsters appear more "attached" to whoever deals the most damage.
  • In party battles, if tanks and attackers keep dealing steady damage, the enemy stays on them more reliably, protecting healers and ranged members. Conversely, the old Provoke-rotation tactics may no longer work as well depending on accumulated damage; controlling damage output between voke tanks - including skillchain damage - becomes necessary. This shift was made to reward more advanced tactics now that players routinely use skillchains.
  • Damage received from lower-level monsters (changed in the October update) has been partially recalibrated. The "Easy Prey" and "Too Weak" thresholds have been adjusted, widening the level range that still gives experience: enemies formerly "Too Weak" have been promoted to "Easy Prey," increasing chances to gain experience from weaker enemies.

Skillchains and Battle

  • Skillchain attributes are now displayed in the log window when a chain activates.
  • Enemy defensive elements now affect skillchain attributes: chains of an element the enemy resists can now be resisted like magic, so some enemies require switching skillchain combinations.
  • Some chain patterns that used to escalate to higher-rank attributes such as Fusion now connect at the same rank instead, changing some chain rules and allowing longer chains.
  • Some notorious monsters now have elemental defense traits that cannot be lowered by Threnody or the new Elemental Seal - a different element or attack method is needed.
  • Behemoth's area attack range has changed. Some monsters' spell choices are now more element-aware. Some monster families' defense and elemental resistances were fine-tuned (affecting that family at all levels). Bomb self-destruct rates have changed.
  • TP gain has been substantially reworked to even out differences between weapon types caused by attack delay, making more weapons viable. TP gained from taking damage has also changed. Stopping a monster's TP move with Weapon Bash or similar now greatly reduces its TP.
  • Magic Bursts with indirect spells are now also announced in the log window; experience chain bonuses are now shown in the log window too.
  • Battle log output has changed to use fewer lines; the double-display of skillchain damage has been fixed; some message colors/filters were reclassified (PS2 font transparency also changed some colors).
  • Magic-detecting monsters no longer react to item use by MP holders - only to actual magic effects.
  • Fixed: with the Martial Arts trait, hand-to-hand delay was stuck at a fixed interval.
  • Focus and Dodge: reuse time cut from 15 to 5 minutes, duration cut from 5 to 2 minutes.
  • Hand-to-hand delay adjusted for jobs without the skill or when boxing with a shield equipped.

Job Additions

  • White Mage: new ability Divine Seal boosts HP recovery of the next cure; stacks additively with healing staves and similar items. Raise II and Reraise II restore 75% of experience lost to KO (targets level 50 or below recover the normal 25%).
  • Black Mage: new ability Elemental Seal raises magic accuracy.
  • Thief: new ability Trick Attack (previously announced as "Blast Attack"): used from hiding behind a party member during battle, it shifts the enemy's targeting onto the ally standing in front of you. "Gil Steal II" has been cancelled after review.
  • Sneak Attack with multi-hit weapon skills now guarantees only the first hit; to compensate, single-hit weapon skill accuracy was raised.
  • Dark Knight/Black Mage: new spell Tractor moves a KO'd player's body to safety. DRK gains the weapon skill Weapon Bash (two-handed weapons only), and Souleater has been strengthened - as a main job, damage now increases with HP lost.
  • Beastmaster: attempting to charm undead or arcana (including beastmen, some giant monsters, and NMs) now has a chance to distract them with an effect equivalent to Bind (no effect on bind-resistant monsters). New ability Reward heals your pet, consuming Pet Food Alpha. Hide now persists while you have a pet. Fixed pets losing claim during Sic; Sic now requires the pet to be fighting.

Travel, Conquest, and Economy

  • Chocobo price inflation now cools faster (prices are still demand-driven).
  • Chocobo rental locations have opened in several places beyond the national stables. Dismounting now asks for confirmation.
  • Conquest now recruits for Expeditionary Forces: after each tally, nations dispatch forces to specific regions. Speak to a gate guard for the participation requirements; with "expeditionary approval" you can teleport to the destination. Contribute by fighting for experience and by opening the region's treasure chests for regional points; HQ issues periodic orders. Success grants individual conquest points to participants.
  • The campaign system letting higher-ranked nations buy lower-ranked nations' reward items for one rank more points is now permanent - speak to that nation's guards at consulates and elsewhere.
  • Rank 6 conquest reward items added. Supply quest conquest points revised; supply quests can now only be taken once per Vana'diel day.
  • Region Info now shows the beastman points of your current region graphically. Signet has a new visual effect.
  • Auction houses now accept single-unit listings of items previously restricted to stacks of 12 or 99. All listing fees and durations changed: fees are now based on each item's shop resale value (stacks pay a flat 10x the single fee) instead of a flat 10 gil; listing duration extended from 3 to 9 Vana'diel weeks (69 Earth hours = 2 days 21 hours).
  • Prices lowered: horo flour, iron ingot, steel ingot, white oak lumber/log, mahogany log/lumber, rosewood lumber/log, Viruna, quill bandana (クイルバンダナ), horn hairpin, hemp gorget, hemp gorget +1. Prices raised: silver ore, silver ingot, gold ingot, gold ore, smoked salmon, salted mushroom (キノコの塩焼き).
  • Now stackable: dhalmel leather, ram leather, flax, lizard skin, baked popoto.
  • Guild shop stock changed at: Fishermen's Guild (Port Windurst), Carpenters' Guild (Northern San d'Oria), Alchemists' Guild (Bastok Mines), Culinarians' Guild (Windurst Waters), Weavers' Guild (Mhaura). Regular shop stock changed in many locations across San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst, Jeuno, and Selbina; a lumber shop opened at the Port San d'Oria airship dock (conquest-linked).
  • The 1st-place nation's chocobo stables now also sell black chocobo feathers.
  • Harvesting point respawn intervals shortened; more item types from logging and mining.
  • Synthesis skill caps raised - but raising any craft beyond 60 requires specialization: as one craft grows past 60, your other crafts decay toward 60 (never below 59). Some equipment can now be desynthesized with lightning crystals, returning a small portion of materials. Alchemy gains new gunpowder recipes for rangers and low-level bullet recipes.

Interface

  • The areas added in October can now be searched with /sea: Behemoth's Dominion, Monastic Cavern, Qu'Bia Arena, Altar Room.
  • The 4th Gobbiebag inventory expansion quest is now available. A quest to expand your Mog House exit has been added, enabling movement such as Port Jeuno to Mog House to Ru'Lude Gardens.
  • Post "discard" and "return" now ask for confirmation; auction proceeds and expired returns can no longer be "returned."
  • Macro editing gains per-button and per-line cut/copy/paste (triangle button or + key).
  • Friend list messages can now be read from within FFXI.
  • Mouse left-click movement is now restricted to 8 directions, making it easier to control.
  • Party members and pet monsters are now shown in distinct colors on the compass.
  • In /party chat, <call> sends party members a whistle and (with a vibration controller) a rumble; patterns <call0> through <call3>. The sound is deliberately jarring - use sparingly and with your party's consent.
  • A network control GUI appears under Config > Global but cannot be used yet.
  • Windows version fixes: possible problem when pressing ONLINE DATA FILE on the top menu; vibration during fishing; Rent-a-Room furniture placement issues; item sorting corruption after area changes; and others.
