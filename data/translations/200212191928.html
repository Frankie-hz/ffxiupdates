• With file checking moved to the PlayOnline Viewer, the "additional patch" and "File check" buttons have been removed from the FINAL FANTASY XI top menu.
  • An auto party matching command has been added to party formation. It automatically assembles parties from players who have flagged themselves; if there aren't enough members in the area, the search widens to the region. No confirmation is asked when forming this way, and the leader is chosen by the system. It cannot be used in some areas (ships, airships, and other places where the Mog House or region map is unavailable); changing areas while flagged cancels the flag.
  • Following last month, artifact quests for five extra jobs have been added: Paladin, Dark Knight, Beastmaster, Bard, and Ranger.
  • The opening sequences of Northern and Southern San d'Oria have changed. The following San d'Orian missions can now be repeated after completion, with new events added: 「オークの斥候を倒せ」(Smash the Orcish Scouts), 「コウモリ退治」(Bat Hunt), 「子供の救助」(Save the Children), 「ダボイ調査報告」(The Davoi Report), 「ダボイ潜入計画」(The Crystal Spring), 「クリスタルの泉」. If you have one on offer already (other than the first), complete it once and offer it again to see the new event.
  • The Windurst quest 「教材募集」 can now be rewarded by trading the items even during other Orastery quests.
  • In party battles against multiple enemies, unengaged aggressive enemies no longer concentrate so heavily on healers, and enemy aggro reaction range has been halved. Continuing last update's work on targeting logic, an additional restriction keeps targeting from being excessively stable in prolonged battles.
  • Cure enmity is now reduced at low levels, scaling by player level: lightest at level 10 and below, reaching current values by level 50.
  • Paladin's Sentinel now generates enmity. Paladin also gains the ability Cover: while a monster targets a party member, target that member and use Cover, then stand between the monster and them to take their hits for a time.
  • Elemental damage spells now get a small accuracy bonus by tier: II resists less than I, III less than II.
  • Fixed: Reraise II could be cast on others. Mage's Ballad II's effect has been lowered. Victory March's effect has been lowered.
  • Fixed the issues reported after the December 3 update: a display bug with equipment that lowers max MP combined with certain jobs, and bards becoming unable to sing.
  • Reward's reuse time has been shortened from 5 to 3 minutes. Pet Food Beta is now sold, and the HP restored by Pet Food Alpha/Beta now scales with the master's MND.
  • Item changes: Rogue's Poulaines - "Flee duration up" was not working for technical reasons, so Ice Resistance +10 was removed to fix it; Healer's Mitts corrected from Healing magic skill +15 to +10; Bronze Bread price corrected from 0; Riot (ライアット) added effect fire to lightning damage; Heat Rod added effect fire to lightning; Divine Sword added light damage effect; Legionnaire's Knuckles (共和軍団兵制式鉄拳) Attack +3; Holy Maul +1 MND+3, dark resistance +4; Venom Knife +1 D20 delay 190; Life Belt equippable jobs changed.
  • Treasure Coffers are now slightly harder to open with thief's tools or living keys, with a slightly higher trap rate. A new thief-only key item, the skeleton key, suppresses coffer traps better.
  • Gil found in chests now scales with party size: less than before solo, unchanged for two, more for three or more. Chests can also very rarely contain jewels.
  • Mining can now yield various gemstones plus turtle shell, fish scales (がら), and more. Mining, logging, and harvesting points in areas where they were spread too widely now appear in narrower, easier-to-find ranges.
  • Expired supply-run goods are now collected automatically when you talk to a town guard. Expeditionary force success rewards changed.
  • Airship shop stock now migrates between nations with conquest rankings. Bastok's airship shop no longer sells gems - the Goldsmiths' Guild sells them instead.
  • Guild recipe teaching reworked: instead of only recipes near your skill, artisans now show a relative difficulty range - Artisan A shows recipes at or below your skill (high success, no skill-up), Artisan B shows recipes slightly above (reasonable success if no sub-skill needed), Artisan C shows harder recipes above that. This makes it easier to judge appropriate synths, and low-difficulty recipes previously hidden are now visible.
  • New recipes: snowy rolanberry (雪山のロランベリー), gelatin, linen doublet, quarterstaff, wool doublet, Pet Food Beta, beetle knife (ビートルダガー), pirate's gun, black cotehardie, skeleton key. Living key yield changed. Some shop buy/sell prices adjusted.
  • Fishing: an unclaimed catch now auto-releases after 10 seconds (was 45), and the confirm button can also cancel fishing. New high-level freshwater bait: giant shell bug (大ヨロイ蟲).
  • Bazaar tax is now formally levied in Jeuno: 10% in Lower Jeuno and Ru'Lude Gardens, 5% in Upper Jeuno and Port Jeuno (added to price).
  • In crowded spots, you can now slip through other players by moving continuously in one direction.
  • Auction traffic limits added: listed item checks, Mog House post menus, and delivery NPC menus can each be used only once per fixed interval. Thank you for your cooperation in reducing traffic.
  • [Ctrl]+[Alt]+[Shift]+1-8 now switches macro palette sets directly. Diagonal input on menus now registers only the vertical direction to prevent misoperation.
  • Targeting yourself and opening the action command now shows a "Check" button (for reviewing your own bazaar comment, etc.) where "Trade" used to be. To trade, use the main menu Trade button, or target the other player and choose Trade from the action command.
  • PS2 version: message fonts now have outlines for readability against bright backgrounds. Windows version: fixed flickering lights indoors and in front of the Southern San d'Oria auction house with bump mapping on.
