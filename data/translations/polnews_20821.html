Guidance on safer management and use of PlayOnline IDs and Square Enix accounts.

If you use the same account name and password for your PlayOnline ID or Square Enix account as for another company's service, a leak of that information from the other service could allow third parties to log in to your account. Please do not reuse account names and passwords from other services.

To stay safe, also note the following:

  • Do not use easily guessed information in passwords, such as repeated characters, phone numbers, or birth dates.
  • Change your password regularly.
  • Do not use programs that risk introducing malware such as viruses or spyware.
  • Do not carelessly open unexpected mail or attachments, or access suspicious websites.

Using a security token with your Square Enix account dramatically improves protection against unauthorized access; we strongly recommend adopting one if you have not.

As a countermeasure against unauthorized access introduced for FINAL FANTASY XIV, logins are restricted when suspicious access is detected. When FINAL FANTASY XIV login is restricted, "An illegal connection to the server was detected" is displayed, and logins to the PlayOnline ID and FINAL FANTASY XI linked to the same Square Enix account are restricted at the same time. In that case, an email with instructions for lifting the restriction is sent to the address registered as "Email Address 1" on the Square Enix account; reset your password before logging in again.
