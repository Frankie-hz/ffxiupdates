If you intend to apply for world migration, please also confirm the following points. Migration must be applied for per character.

Destination: the number of new worlds is not fixed at application time. Even if migrants end up split across multiple new worlds, applicants from the same origin world will always be moved to the same new world.

Delivery, post, and auction items: items in delivery slots, the Mog House post, and auction house consignments are automatically discarded at migration. Keep such items on the character or in the Mog Safe before the migration date.

Opened linkshells: linkpearls and linksacks cannot be carried to the new world and vanish automatically at migration. Linkshells themselves are exchanged free of charge for a new unopened linkshell on the destination world: when the linkshell owner first logs in on the new world, the old linkshell is gone but a replacement "unopened linkshell" is placed in their inventory automatically. No special procedure is needed, and the unopened linkshell is not subject to deletion.
