Battle and Magic

  • The experience point bonus for party play has been further increased, to the greater advantage of large parties.
  • Experience gained for defeating enemies of a higher level than yourself now increases further according to the level difference.
  • Elemental and other attack magic that strikes an enemy's elemental weakness is now far less likely to be resisted. Choose effective spells with the enemy's weakness, the day's element, and the weather in mind. Attacks with elements other than the weakness behave as before.
  • To assist with this, when the clock display is on, the current day's element and weather element are now shown on the compass at the lower left of the screen. The clock and day/weather display toggles each time you select the current time in the menu. Certain weather is strongly aligned with an element; in that case two weather element icons are shown side by side, indicating the weather's influence is stronger. (The effects of weather and day elements themselves have existed all along.)
  • The rate at which enfeebling magic such as Sleep, Blind, and Bind is resisted due to level difference with the enemy has been relaxed.
  • The following new battle songs have been added for Bard:
    • Elemental resistance-lowering songs: Fire Threnody, Ice Threnody, Wind Threnody, Earth Threnody, Lightning Threnody, Water Threnody, Light Threnody, Dark Threnody
    • Enhancing-magic removal song: Magic Finale
  • The three white magic Teleport spells and the black magic Warp II are now unlocked. Each Teleport requires attunement with the corresponding gate crystal. The white magic spells Shellra and Protectra II are also now obtainable.
  • Curative magic now draws enemy attention somewhat more than before. Take care when using healing magic during battle.
  • The duration of the warrior job ability Mighty Strikes is now 1.5 times what it was.

Interface

  • During battle you can now use the triangle button to select past logs, status icons, and so on, just as outside battle. In reactive-size window mode, the X button can temporarily reduce the number of displayed lines even during battle.
  • When viewing past logs, pressing the triangle button now provides simple filtering that shows only selected chat channels. The viewable range is somewhat limited while filtering.
  • The compass now automatically tracks the nearest monster for which a Call for Help has been raised, making it easier to go to the rescue.
  • Your current block coordinates can now be expressed in chat with the pronoun <pos>.
  • A confirmation window now appears when refusing a call from the friend list.
  • While selecting an area in Search, pressing the triangle button switches to region selection mode; choosing a region lists only the areas in that region. Press triangle again to return to the full area list.
  • Search results now color-code players who are already in a party.

New Content

  • Mining is now possible at mines in various places. At mining points in Zeruhn, Palborough, Gusgen, and other mines, use a pickaxe from the item list (or trade one) on a Mining Point for a chance to obtain various ores, including rare ones. Each Mining Point is good for one excavation, after which it moves elsewhere.
  • Mysterious treasure chests now appear deep within some dungeons. A key is needed to open them. Once opened, a chest disappears and reappears elsewhere after a time. In a party, chest contents are treated as spoils that party members can lot on.
  • Several new quests have been added. (Note: the planned quest to expand the Mog Safe was postponed to the next version update around August 20 due to a bug found during adjustment.)
  • The rewards and completion requirements of many existing quests have been changed, mostly increasing rewards and lowering difficulty. Affected quests include (Japanese titles): 貴婦人の所望, 平穏なる日々, 灯台もと暗し, 心の壁, 砂丘に舞う光, 臥竜の滝, 特ダネ・ウィンダス, 伝説の大作戦, 夜空はコイン, 3本の杖, 補給物資, ライバル, 工房長の秘密, ゴキゲンナナメな機械, インソムニアの女, 黒の技.
  • NPCs who tell you your adventurer fame have been placed in each city. Quest availability in each nation depends on your fame as an adventurer, not on your number of completed quests. Fame rises faster from difficult quests and one-time-only quests, and can also change based on quest outcomes.
  • NPCs who change your title have been placed in each city, for use when a quest has left you with an unwanted title.

Other

  • Fixed a bug where high-grade fish that should not be catchable were rarely caught by low-skill anglers. Fishing skill now also rises more easily than before.
  • When a synthesis uses multiple copies of the same item, the quantity can now be specified in one slot; you no longer need to manually split stacked items beforehand.
  • The Status > Synthesis skill list now shows current skill points along with current rank. When using a crystal, the display changes to a skill-points-only list.
  • Synthesis is now possible inside your Mog House.
  • Job changes in the Mog House now happen on the spot without a screen transition. The old job change screen can no longer be seen.
  • The display of winning bids sent from the auction house when checking your Mog House post has changed, and the icons in the post are easier to read.
  • Chocobo rental fees vary with recent usage, but riders under level 50 now receive a discount scaled to the level difference; a level 20 rider gets the maximum 30% off.
  • New reward items have been added to Conquest. These new rewards appear depending on national conquest rankings.
  • The "San d'Orian salute" motion has been re-implemented for female Tarutaru from San d'Oria (/salute).
  • The ONLINE DATA FILE is now usable in the Windows beta version.
  • As announced, new high-quality results are now possible for existing recipes. The addition of brand-new recipes has been postponed to a later version update; timing will be announced separately.
  • August's special event is planned for August 24-25. You can join, pause, or leave at any time during the period; all levels are welcome and party size is free. Details will be announced soon.
