We have received many inquiries about an error displayed after entering credit card information during the transition to a Square Enix account. From the inquiries received, the error is displayed for the following reasons:

  • The credit card does not support 3-D Secure identity verification.
  • The card supports 3-D Secure, but the application or configuration required to use it has not been completed (this must be done with the card company).
  • A problem has occurred with the card itself.

If an error appears after entering card information, please confirm with your card company that the card supports 3-D Secure and that the verification service is enabled and usable, and that there are no problems with the card.
