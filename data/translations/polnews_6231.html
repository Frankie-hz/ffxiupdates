As of December 9, 2005, the following limitations and phenomena have been confirmed when playing the Xbox 360 version. They do not obstruct progress within the scope of the beta test and will be fixed during development toward the retail version.

Limitations:

  • Operation of the Xbox 360 wireless LAN adapter with PlayOnline and FINAL FANTASY XI has not been verified.
  • USB peripherals other than keyboards (e.g. USB mice) cannot be used with FINAL FANTASY XI.
  • Keyboard settings depend on the Xbox 360 dashboard settings; using an English keyboard requires setting the dashboard language to English.

USB keyboard issues: full-width roman letters cannot be entered; the F9/F10 conversion keys do not work; numbers cannot be entered on the numeric keypad; the Num Lock and Caps Lock lamps do not light; fixed alphanumeric input does not work with Caps Lock on during full-width input; some symbol key assignments in the in-game keyboard config may differ from the key tops (opening the Xbox Guide keyboard once works around this).

Other: the GM call queue count displays incorrectly (GMs see the correct count - please keep waiting); BGM may not play while riding a chocobo; temporary items cannot be used in Ballista; friend invitations register only on the inviter's list (the invitee inviting back registers both); PlayOnline and the Vana'diel Tribune cannot be visited from the main menu or Mog House (exit the game and view them in PlayOnline); screenshots save at a 4:3 aspect ratio; the support desk lacks a "cannot move" option under progress-blocking problems (use another option to GM call); the character creation screens and the missing opening movie are provisional in the beta.
