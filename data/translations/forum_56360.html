Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 49." The broadcast centered on the January version update. Summary below.

FFXI in 2020 (00:03:42)

As the year's first broadcast, we discussed the outlook. Last year laid groundwork toward the 20th anniversary - installer overhaul and smoother onboarding for new players. This year is about stacking content for a fulfilling Vana'diel life on top of that. One piece is the Wanted equipment reforging mentioned in the New Year's greeting, with new content planned around it. New content brings congestion problems, but we intend a wide-entrance operation using the instance layering developed for Ambuscade last year. The Wanted-reforge content should be joinable from around CL119 at its introduction and grow more challenging through expansion; rewards will be commensurate, around Ambuscade equipment +2 level. Accessories are also planned to be reforgeable, further widening equipment choices.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (00:12:38): Volume 1 is Antica; Volume 2 is Clots - New Year's mochi-themed. Landing weapon skills tears off pieces of the mochi (spawning adds); repeat while clearing adds and the main body becomes easier to defeat. Among the adds, an otoshidama (New Year's gift) monster can appear - best NOT to kill that one.
  • A wee job adjustment: Beastmaster (00:16:01) ("wee" to match its scale): Ready move activation time against enemies is now uniform and shorter, making it easier to time weapon skills for skillchains. Self-enhancing pet moves now also grant their effect to the master (Rhino Guard and Fantod excluded for balance). Merit adjustment: each merit in "Sic Recast" now also shortens Ready's recast by 2 seconds - the floor doesn't change, but gear slots previously needed for recast can now go to other stats.
  • Domain Invasion (00:23:54): after tuning it for solo/small groups, domain points became hard to earn with a crowd. Fixes: add-spawn wait time now shortens to a minimum of 30 seconds as participation grows; NM HP now scales up with participant count (finely stepped so it doesn't become oppressive); and "domain points gained / held" now print in the defeat log, since traveling to Norg to check was a chore.
