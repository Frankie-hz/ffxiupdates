Beta tester recruitment for "Fantasy Earth: The Ring of Dominion" began Monday, September 26!

Ahead of the retail release, PlayOnline plans to run a beta test of this entirely new style of online game. Beta testers will test-play the beta version to identify and fix problems.

The exhilaration of an action game charging into massed armies, the fun of growing alongside comrades in an MMORPG, and the depth of real-time strategy in assembling a war - a chance to experience a new world of online gaming before anyone else. Apply now!

See "Fantasy Earth" under Games in the main menu or the official site (http://www.playonline.com/fe/) for recruitment details. Fantasy Earth is a Windows-only online game.
