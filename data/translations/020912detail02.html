What Is Gardening?

Place a flowerpot in your Mog House layout and plant a seed in it to begin gardening. Once started, the plant grows slowly over time under your Moogle's care, eventually blooming to delight the eye. Keep raising it and the plant will begin to glow, bearing various items as fruit!

Items Used in Gardening

  • Gardening always requires a flowerpot and a seed. Both are managed by your Moogle, so the needed items must be deposited in your Mog Safe.
  • Flowerpots are sold in shops. Every nation sells them, but the material differs by nation. Plants grow healthily in any material, but for some harvests the yield can differ. Up to 10 flowerpots can be placed in your Mog House. They cannot be placed in Rent-a-Rooms, so to place a new pot you must return to your home nation's Mog House.
  • Seeds seem to be carried by plant-type monsters. Since the plant type can only be judged from the seed's shape, they are generally called "mysterious ... seeds." Plant one per flowerpot to start growth.
  • Crystals are not required, but can be used as fertilizer to invigorate a weakened plant. Feeding a crystal can change growth speed and harvest results.

Growth Stages

  • Seed: just planted; nothing visible yet.
  • Sprouting: the seed has sprouted and greenery appears. Depending on the seed, there is a risk of wilting at this stage.
  • Growth: the sprout stretches out branches and leaves. The risk of wilting is ever-present.
  • Flowering: growth advances and beautiful flowers appear, signaling that maturity is near.
  • Maturity: the flowers give off a phosphorescent glow - the plant's most beautiful moment. Harvesting is possible.
  • Withered: the plant's end. Once withered, there is no way to revive it.

Care Commands

  • Plant a seed: plants a seed in the pot. The seed must be stored in your Mog Safe beforehand. Once planted, a seed cannot be recovered.
  • Check on it: examines the plant's condition (from a Rent-a-Room, your Moogle checks for you). Checking can prevent the plant from withering, so check at least once a day.
  • Dry it: turns the plant into a dried flower, fixing its appearance. A dried plant no longer grows or withers - good for keeping as room decor.
  • Feed a crystal: when the plant is weak, feeds it a crystal from your Mog Safe as fertilizer. As a side effect, crystal-fed plants may produce items not normally possible - the plant will not wither from it, but the influence is not guaranteed to be positive, and growth speed can change drastically for some plants.
  • Harvest: when the plant glows softly, it is ready. Harvesting yields some kind of item - occasionally, perhaps from unbalanced nutrition, even things that do not normally grow on plants. Harvesting removes the plant from the pot; the harvest goes into your Mog Safe. If the Mog Safe is full, the plant stays as it is without being harvested.
  • Stop gardening: abandons cultivation and removes the plant from the pot.

Gardening from a Rent-a-Room

Care can also be performed from a Rent-a-Room. Your Moogle travels to your Mog House by its own magic and checks for you; choose commands based on its reports. Planting and harvesting work the same way. Restrictions: flowerpots cannot be placed or re-arranged from a Rent-a-Room, and you cannot see the plants with your own eyes. Close the gardening menu and the Moogle returns to your side.

Growth While Logged Out

Plants keep growing even while you are logged out of Vana'diel. There is no need to stay logged in for long periods.
