As previously announced, we have confirmed "phishing" fraud in which players are led to pages impersonating the Square Enix Account Management System (https://secure.square-enix.com/account/) and asked to enter their Square Enix account, password, and birth date so the information can be stolen.

Recently we have also confirmed pages impersonating our Support Center.

Phishing sites request your Square Enix account and password. The genuine Account Management System uses an EV SSL certificate: on a supporting browser, the address bar turns green and shows the operating organization "SQUARE ENIX CO., LTD.". Always confirm this organization name is displayed before entering your account and password.

When entering your account and password via the Support Center, a genuine site transitions to the Account Management System; if the organization name "SQUARE ENIX CO., LTD." is not displayed on the entry screen, the site is fake.

No page other than the login page displaying "SQUARE ENIX CO., LTD." ever asks for your password, and our staff never ask for your password.

If you have accessed a suspicious site or entered your account and password on one, change your password immediately and run a virus/spyware scan with security software. To further protect your account, we recommend the Square Enix Security Token (one-time password).
