Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 56," covering the July and August version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (July/August) (00:05:01): Volume 1 is Sahagin (revival); Volume 2 is Funguars. Funguars multiply while a Dark Elemental is present; defeating the elemental to "bring in the light" makes the Funguars easier to damage, so push during those windows. August's Volume 1 debuts Ramuh as a new monster - Volume 1 foes were traditionally beastmen, but its concept of each party member fulfilling their role works for other monsters too, so we chose Ramuh; his staff joins the exchange rewards. Volume 2 is still in tuning but is planned to feature an adjusted Citrullus.
  • High-tier battlefield: Shinryu (00:11:38): newly added, tuned to today's adventurer strength without atmas or the old support effects. Entry conditions were covered; note the Phantom Quartz [Shinryu] exchange rate will differ from the figure stated on air. Some spoils were previewed on video.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (August) (00:23:05): no story addition in July, but the Menagerie Receiver's (メネジンレシーバー) reception area expands. The fifth arc begins in August.
  • Other July changes (00:25:05): Some items' NPC buyback price exceeded their purchase price, so after weighing the economic impact (and to avoid making Pilgrim Moogle sales mandatory), the Pilgrim Moogle's buyback prices no longer fluctuate; items temporarily removed from the shop lineup return in the July update. Due to patch server maintenance, July's file check takes longer than usual - roughly 15-30 minutes on HDDs, 3-4 minutes on SSDs. A fix was applied for reduced performance on certain CPU/GPU combinations: mainly on laptops, graphics work previously done on the CPU is now routed to the GPU.
