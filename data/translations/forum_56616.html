Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 50." The broadcast centered on the March version update. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (00:06:34): Volume 1 is Velkk; Volume 2 is Tulfaires, styled as warblers heralding spring with a winter-into-spring gimmick. Elemental adds appear in the order ice, wind, then light - winter, the first spring gale, then spring light. The boss starts very tough, but defeating elementals advances the season, and by the time light elementals appear your damage flows nicely. You'll generally kill all elementals as you go, but leaving one light elemental alive might bring something good.
  • Domain Invasion (00:09:20): various feedback-driven adjustments (details in the version update notes). Intent behind the high-tier NM changes: respawn lottery interval changed from 60 minutes to 5 days (they're meant to be powerful, so quick respawns were harsh); evasion/magic evasion sharply lowered (they were tuned assuming full food and buff preparation, which doesn't fit casual group play - if you still can't hit, adjust food and gear); domain point base gains now grow with the consecutive-kill count, tallied per world, as a reward for repeat clears; the content level now prints in the log when one spawns; a "battle preparation" invincibility status is granted on entry (players were being hit before enemies rendered); and the "Awakening" status gains accuracy/magic accuracy bonuses so attacks connect - spend your gear budget on other stats. Display priority was also adjusted so high-tier NMs stay targetable in crowds; please post feedback on how it feels in practice.
  • New Trust: Monberaux (00:23:20): announced at the summer offline meetup, now implemented. As the "chemist-style" description implied, he is a healer who consumes no MP, restoring HP, MP, and status ailments - especially valuable in long fights where Kupipi would run dry. Since he heals with his own items, silence and amnesia cannot shut him down; only "cannot use items" stops him. After obtaining him, enjoy the follow-up event in Upper Jeuno.
  • New battle content: Odyssey (00:29:07): an exploration-style battle content set in Walk of Echoes where Wanted +1 equipment can be reinforced. No entry prerequisites; also usable for job point grinding (participation and occupancy details in the update notes). The name comes from Homer's Odyssey - it is meant to be played again and again rather than completed once. Early-area monsters are around level 119 but hit harder than that suggests, with correspondingly high experience. From the live play session: regular monsters yield experience, reforge materials, and Izzat (a content-specific point); they don't respawn, and the monster mix is random per entry; "Nostos" monsters do not link, so they can be pulled one at a time. Izzat opens locked chests and, in certain cases, spawns NMs (some NMs pop from traded items instead). Examining the veridical conflux stones moves you deeper; the deeper you go, the stronger the enemies and the more job points. One of the Wanted-reforge conditions is the Records of Eminence objective for reaching the goal of Sheol A, so push for the deepest point. Sheol B and beyond, dedicated bosses, and reward equipment are planned for future updates.

This Month's Announcements (01:12:45)

  • Spring Equipment Campaign: another month of support for players still working on obtaining or +1-ing Wanted equipment.
  • March 2020 Login Campaign: headline item is the Mutsunokami; obtain two and synergize them into the Mutsunokami Kai.

Upcoming Updates (01:15:02)

  • New rewards for rerun events: events like "The Archduke Wants Attention Again" and "Shadow Lord Day" will get new rewards so past clearers can enjoy them again - we can't say what yet, but things of a different flavor from existing rewards, such as orchestrion rolls, are under consideration.
  • New furnishings: a Moogle bed, a chocobo chest of drawers, and other upcoming furnishings were shown on video.
  • New scenario content: Yaeko Sato, scenario writer of the Windurst and Chains of Promathia missions, joins to write new story content. Look forward to it.
