Until now it has not been possible, for various reasons, to open a test server to FINAL FANTASY XI players. However, thanks to test program preparation by the development team and the cooperation of the FINAL FANTASY XI PLAY ZONE "REAL VANA'DIEL" (operated by Melco Online Entertainment, http://www.realvanadiel.jp/), a login environment to a test server has been arranged - as a limited event restricted in days, headcount, and location - where version update content can be experienced in advance.

We are therefore recruiting a small number of participants to test the Dynamis ("shadow world") content planned for the next version update.

For how to apply, dates, and details, see "Public Test Participants Wanted!" in the fan book on the FINAL FANTASY XI top page.

The purpose of the test is large-scale load testing and large-party battle balance testing. It runs over two days, connecting to the test server from REAL VANA'DIEL.
