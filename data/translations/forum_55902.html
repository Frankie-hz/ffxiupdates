Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 47," broadcast this time from the Dengeki no Ryodan "FFXI Summer Festival Offline Meetup 2019." Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Job adjustments: Bard (0:02:05): Following Red Mage in August, the merit-point group songs have been converted to scrolls. Since bards often move close to enemies to distribute songs, a new job trait reducing physical and magic damage taken was added as the replacement merit category. Scroll-converted songs behave as if 5/5 merited, and augment effects were changed to match the new categories. Remaining jobs will be converted in turn.
  • Ambuscade (0:05:11): September brings a Shadow revival and Mantises. The Volume 2 Mantis fight is now a simple one-on-one rather than the previous multi-enemy battle. Its signature move Death Prophet (KO effect) can be survived by receiving it while healing (resting), but since many players run Ambuscade with Trusts, the move's name and effect have been changed. The boss shifts through three modes by remaining HP, using a special move at each shift - handling those is the key.
  • High-tier mission battlefield: Lady Lilith (0:10:16): fight while avoiding the circles that appear. On Normal difficulty and above there is "a certain change" - players who fought Lady Lilith in the Wings of the Goddess missions may guess what. Rewards were shown in the video.
  • Monster rearing: Mandragoras (0:16:03): finally! Besides the four varieties shown, the New Year's one is also rearable.
  • Other updates (0:17:27): Augmented equipment becomes deliverable within the same account - rolled out gradually because many items need verification; September covers Reisenjima geas fete equipment. Stack limits for Abdhaljs seals and various coupons rise to 99. (Items listable on the auction house were requested, but those require halting the AH to adjust, so they were not included this time.) Full details in the version update notes.

Looking Further Ahead

  • New Trust: Monberaux (0:26:29): the "???" Deed reward revealed. As befits a doctor, we hope to implement Monberaux as a character who heals without magic - think of the Chemist in FINAL FANTASY V.
  • Unity: Wanted equipment reforging (0:28:27): +1 pieces will accept augments - not random, but a single upgrade path.
  • Domain Invasion rework (0:30:39): it's harsh with few players and over in an instant with many, so the balance needs revisiting. Planning is under way, aiming for a satisfying pace, easy entry for small groups and returnees, and rewards that, like Ambuscade's Hallmarks, reliably scale with how much you play.
  • New avatar: Siren (0:32:38): preparations continue for Siren as a new avatar, shown on video, and the acquisition quest is being carefully built.
  • White Mage hoodie (0:35:05): a hoodie inspired by the White Mage garb is in planning, designed by Ryoma Ito (of FINAL FANTASY TACTICS ADVANCE fame), aiming for everyday-wearable quality.

The next Freshly Picked Vana'diel is planned for November. See you then!
