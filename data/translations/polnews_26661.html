With the lowering of the age of majority on Friday, April 1, 2022, the age-based payment limits change as follows: the payment limit for users aged 18-19 is abolished; limits for users under 18 are unchanged.

Limits after the change: under 16 - up to 5,000 yen per month; 16-17 - up to 30,000 yen per month; 18 and over - no limit.

Age is counted monthly, reaching the new age the month after the birthday month. For system reasons, users aged 18-19 remain subject to the 30,000 yen monthly limit until the Square Enix account maintenance on April 1, 2022 ends (planned 15:00).
