During PlayOnline Viewer and FINAL FANTASY XI version updates, the following error messages may appear and prevent the client software from updating:

  • "POL-0010": the connection failed because server access is concentrated. Please wait a while and try again.
  • "POL-1160" (Windows version): the connection may be failing due to security features such as your PC's firewall. Because the client software was updated, the firewall recognizes the PlayOnline Viewer as new software. Reconfigure your firewall; see your security software's manual for the method.
