Since the start of FINAL FANTASY XI Treasures of Aht Urhgan service, a prolonged problem has been occurring in which expansion content cannot be registered.

The problem is caused by concentrated access to the registration information management server used for expansion registration, whose load has greatly exceeded the server's processing capacity. The concentration has also substantially degraded the processing power of the registration server equipment overall, which is a further factor behind the registration backlog.

To relieve the congestion we have been reinforcing the registration servers and repeatedly tuning the server programs, and these measures are gradually increasing the number of registrations processed per hour. However, it is expected to take more time before the congestion is fully resolved.

Retrying registration immediately after an error not only fails but worsens the congestion, making registration even harder for everyone. If you receive an error, please wait at least 10 to 20 minutes before trying again.

Telephone lines to our Information Center are extremely congested and we are unable to provide guidance by phone. Updates on the situation will be posted under "Information" as circumstances change.

Our staff are doing everything possible to restore normal service as quickly as possible. We deeply apologize for the prolonged inconvenience.
