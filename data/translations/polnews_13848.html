Today we opened the "Unauthorized Access Chat Support Desk," a new dedicated contact point for inquiries about unauthorized account access.

From now on, please direct unauthorized access inquiries to this chat support desk rather than calling the Information Center. Other inquiries can continue to use the Information Center phone lines or GM calls in game.

The desk is a chat-based support system usable in a web browser.

Before contacting support, please review the information on countermeasures against unauthorized access linked in the original notice.
