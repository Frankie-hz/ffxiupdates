The wait is over! From noon today, Thursday, May 12, the retail service of FRONT MISSION ONLINE has begun.

FRONT MISSION ONLINE is an online game set in the Second Huffman Conflict - the same setting as FRONT MISSION 1st - in which each player becomes a single soldier cooperating with allies against the enemy force. Player characters join either the O.C.U. or U.S.N. army and take part in the conflict. Every player's battles are reflected in real time in the state of the war on Huffman Island; gathering comrades to face the enemy yields greater results.

As already announced, a client version update program must be downloaded before play; see the related information. Also see the supplements to the bundled "Playing Manual 2005" on the FRONT MISSION ONLINE top page under Play Guide, on the official site, and in Topics.
