Regarding WebMoney period-extension procedures: access concentrates on the last day of the month, and the procedure may fail. Please avoid the last day of the month when extending content IDs.

Customers who need to extend are shown a notice at PlayOnline login when an eligible paid content ID exists; follow the on-screen instructions to renew. Extensions can also be performed in the PlayOnline Viewer under Service & Support, "Member Information", "Payment Method", "WebMoney Extension".

Additionally, from today, customers who need to extend are shown guidance at PlayOnline login about extensions for the following month onward.
