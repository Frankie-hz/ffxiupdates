The Vana'diel Tribune has begun accepting information from adventurers! Are there interesting happenings or events around you? Send in your "scoops"! We also welcome reports from linkshell representatives.

Information received will be used in producing the Vana'diel Tribune. We are also still recruiting volunteer correspondents to help edit the paper with reporting and writing.

The Vana'diel Tribune can be viewed from: the Topics top page, the FINAL FANTASY XI fan book, and the Mog House in game. The scoop submission and linkshell survey forms are not available on the playonline.com version of the Tribune.
