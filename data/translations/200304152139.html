All Users

  • The first page of the login screen has been removed; login now starts at the character-select menu. The per-area ONLINE DATA FILE ends with this update (the Vana'diel Tribune and albums remain reachable from the fan book on the top page). The in-game "DATA FILE" button has been replaced by "Mog Safe check," which lets you view (but not sort or withdraw) your safe contents outside the Mog House. The /datafile text command has been removed.
  • The new extra job Summoner has been unsealed. Only a few low-tier summoning spells can be obtained without the expansion; avatars found in Rise of the Zilart areas are for expansion users, though the eight elemental spirit summons can be traded and sold as scrolls. Summoner traits and abilities: Max MP Boost (Lv10), Resist Slow (Lv20), Auto Refresh (Lv25), and the SP ability Astral Flow (Lv1).
  • The job level cap has been raised to 65; clear limit break quest 3, "Whence Blows the Wind." Combat skill caps rise with level, unlocking new weapon skills, and synthesis caps rise as well.
  • For parties with members at level 61+, experience now reflects your own level versus the enemy more strongly, and inter-member level difference corrections are greatly relaxed.

Job Adjustments

  • Warrior: Aggressor no longer boosts ranged accuracy; its reuse time has changed.
  • Monk: new ability Chi Blast; new trait Kick Attacks (occasional kicks during auto-attack with hand-to-hand equipped); Guard skill cap up; Martial Arts revised - upgrades every 15 levels, slightly weaker below level 30 but stronger above; Staff skill cap up.
  • White Mage: levels 61-65 white magic unlocked; new exclusive spell Erase (removes one enfeebling effect from an ally); enfeebling skill cap up.
  • Black Mage: levels 61-65 black magic unlocked; Flare, Freeze, Tornado, Quake, Burst, and Flood now leave a lingering elemental effect on the target that lowers resistance to the opposing element (fire leaves an effect that weakens resistance to water, etc.), removed after a time or when the opposing element lands; Staff skill cap up.
  • Red Mage: new ability Convert (instantly swaps current HP and MP, capped at maximum); levels 61-65 white magic unlocked; new exclusive spells Phalanx (reduces damage taken) and Dispel (removes one magic effect from the enemy); Fast Cast revised (always shortens casting); Marksmanship skill removed; Sword skill cap up.
  • Thief: Trick Attack from a support job no longer receives the stat bonus to damage.
  • Paladin: new ability Rampart (raises defense of party members in range); Throwing skill removed.
  • Dark Knight: new exclusive spells Absorb-STR, Absorb-DEX, Absorb-VIT, Absorb-AGI, Absorb-INT, Absorb-MND, Absorb-CHR (each drains that stat from the enemy).
  • Beastmaster: new ability Call Beast (consumes a beastmaster jug item in the ammo slot to summon a pet); Charm revised - with BST as support job, monsters above your support level are harder to charm; gains Scythe skill.
  • Bard: refresh songs (Mage's Ballad line) slightly weakened; haste songs (Advancing March line) slightly weakened; Soul Voice shortened to 3 minutes.
  • Ranger: new ability Unlimited Shot (the next ranged attack consumes no ammunition).

New and Unlocked Magic

Magic and Battle

  • Skill-up rates for magic cast on enemies and for ranged attacks have been raised.
  • -ga elemental spells now deal more damage, but damage per target varies with the number of enemies caught (per-target power can drop to about 40%); their MP costs are uniformly lower.
  • The following spells now scale with the caster/target stat difference and skill: Slow, Paralyze, Burn, Frost, Choke, Rasp, Shock, Drown, Drain (MP cost also lowered), Aspir, Blind.
  • Gravity now also slightly lowers the target's evasion (around 5%).
  • Invisible, Sneak, and Deodorize can no longer be overwritten before they fully expire - wait for the effect to end or cancel it yourself first. Items with these effects behave the same way.
  • Changing jobs now resets effects such as Reraise. SP ability reuse timers are no longer reset by job changes - they now belong to the character, so one character can use an SP ability only once every 2 hours regardless of job changing.
  • Item use no longer consumes the item if you move during activation; however, non-scroll items count as consumed if paralysis stops their effect. Magic scrolls are only consumed by learning them.
  • Fixed: Regen ticks were waking sleeping targets (cures and damage still do). Fixed: repeatedly cancelling Tractor and Raise while KO'd could break later Raise offers until relogging. Battlefield monsters and beastman-boss-class monsters now see through Invisible, Sneak, and Deodorize.
  • Skillchains: each additional step now shortens the window to continue the chain (this also affects Magic Bursts). After a weapon skill used under the samurai ability Meikyo Shisui, the window is extended back to the initial length. Level 3 skillchains cannot be extended further. A damage cap has been set on skillchain damage. Chain rule changes: Freezebite to Sunburst and Freezebite to Starburst now form Compression rather than Gravitation; Rampage can no longer chain into Rampage; Penta Thrust can no longer chain into Penta Thrust.
  • Swapping some ranged weapons or ammo slot items now also resets TP.

World Changes

  • New blocks open in these dungeons: Toraimarai Canal, Delkfutt's Tower, King Ranperre's Tomb, Bostaunieux Oubliette. Treasure Coffer spawn points changed in some dungeons. Chest and coffer keys now drop randomly from all monsters of the appropriate level band in every dungeon, instead of specific monsters.
  • Orcs of level 30+ have more max HP. Dhalmel hide and bone drop rates are lower.
  • Auction fees revised: Bastok/San d'Oria/Windurst/Norg/Rabao - base 1 gil + 1/100 of shop value (unchanged); Jeuno/Kazham - base 100 gil + 1/20 of shop value. The former 100 gil cap on fees for items valued 10,000+ gil is abolished. Shop resale prices have generally fallen. Stack listings (12 or 99) now cost 5x the single fee (was 10x).
  • World passes now admit one new player per pass. (A world pass, bought in-game, lets someone create a new character on that world.)
  • Search comments now show a green dot before the player's name and display in green in the log. Search categories added for mission ranks 7-10 and the new extra jobs.
  • Windows version: a "background screen area" slider was added under Config > Misc. 2 for non-4:3 displays; actual resolution still follows FFXI Config's back buffer setting.
  • The auto-offline network setting now syncs with the PlayOnline Viewer setting; the default is 20 minutes, which disconnects after 20 idle minutes - change it if undesired.
  • The penalty time a character remains in the world after a sudden disconnection has been extended from 30 seconds to 1 minute, to counter players deliberately pulling the plug to escape enemies. Unintentional disconnections unfortunately incur the same penalty, as the two cannot be distinguished.
  • Macro sets 9 and 10 (Ctrl1-0, Alt1-0, 40 macros) have been added.
  • Pet HP is now shown while selecting pet commands, and pet commands can be reached from the Abilities menu.
  • Expeditionary force regional points and rewards have been substantially revised.
  • With Rise of the Zilart, the regions Li'Telor, Kuzotz, Vollbow, Elshimo Lowlands, and Elshimo Uplands join Conquest, and new Zilart areas join existing regions - 16 conquest regions in all (was 11), changing alliance campaign conditions.
  • Map/region screens: hold R2 (Alt) to scroll faster; R3 (Ctrl+V or numpad 5) centers the region cursor or resets map scale; cursor range has been widened.
  • Trading crystals to Conquest Guards now yields rank points scaled by your rank. Jeuno's regional goods shop has closed. Some (mostly stackable) foods now have shorter effect durations. New items from logging, mining, and harvesting, including in the new Zilart areas.
  • Reward/difficulty changes to quests in San d'Oria (甘い蜜の誘惑, 見習い登用試験2, 武器の仕入れ, 森の仲買人), Windurst (航海帽子, クロウラーを狩るもの), Bastok (クラウツ橋の隣人, 黒の技, ライバル, 釣りとバケツの関係, 買い取りなら天晶堂), and Selbina (本当のプレゼント).
  • Fixed: water and ice element colors were swapped on the equipment screen and magic icons (water is blue, ice is light blue); day colors and the elemental wheel were already correct.
  • The Mithran fishing rod is now sold by the Fishermen's Guild. Left-clicking now also cancels fishing. Fixed: the default chat color for yourself and others was identical. Friend requests now default the friend name to the requester's character name; double blacklisting and accepting requests from blacklisted players are now blocked; blacklisting no longer requires the player to be in the same area; empty friend-list messages no longer display a body.
  • Fixed: /equip failing when the same item appears on two consecutive macro lines. Fixed: <stpc> targets not cycling with the directional buttons. <stnpc> now starts from a nearby NPC rather than yourself (and aborts if none is in range). <pet> now returns your own pet's name rather than the nearest pet. <bt> no longer points at defeated monsters. The new pronoun <ht> returns the nearest monster with a Call for Help raised.
  • Sub-target initial position now prefers your current target: yourself for self-castable spells like Cure, the current enemy for offensive magic, yourself if no target, otherwise the nearest valid target. /attack on a non-enemy target now returns an error. If a bazaar item is bought by someone else mid-purchase, the window now closes. Discarding Rare/Ex items now asks for confirmation.

Rise of the Zilart Expansion Users Only

  • The new extra jobs Samurai, Ninja, and Dragoon become available by clearing their respective quests.
    • Samurai - traits: Resist Blind (Lv5), Store TP (Lv10), Demon Killer (Lv40); SP ability Meikyo Shisui (Lv1, weapon skills cost a flat 100 TP); abilities: Warding Circle (Lv5), Third Eye (Lv15), Meditate (Lv30).
    • Ninja - traits: Stealth (Lv5), Resist Bind (Lv10), Dual Wield (Lv10); SP ability Mijin Gakure (Lv1, sacrifices your life to damage the enemy, with no experience loss).
    • Dragoon - traits: Attack Bonus (Lv10), Dragon Killer (Lv25), Accuracy Bonus (Lv30); SP ability Call Wyvern (Lv1); abilities: Ancient Circle (Lv5), Jump (Lv10), High Jump (Lv35), Super Jump (Lv50, leap out of the enemy's targeting entirely).
  • Missions from Rank 6 onward are unlocked.
  • The long-suspended Kazham-Jeuno airship route resumes from Port Jeuno; expansion users only.
  • The new outlands have no Mog Houses; instead, Nomad Moogles at each outpost handle Mog Safe access, post checks, and job changes through their own moogle network (no gardening or layout). You must move your Mog House address to use them; changing jobs in the outlands cancels auto party matching flags.
  • Outland towns have no formal auction houses; local brokers connect to the national markets: Kazham = Windurst market, Rabao = San d'Oria market, Norg = Bastok market. To protect the brokers, outland towns levy bazaar taxes: Kazham 15%, Rabao 10%, Norg 5%.
