Support arrangements for the Information Center and game masters over the year-end and New Year holidays:

  • Information Center (phone and chat support): normal 11:00-19:00 operation through Friday, December 26, 2008; closed Saturday, December 27, 2008 through Sunday, January 4, 2009; normal operation resumes Monday, January 5 (excluding weekends and holidays).
  • Mail support: mail received during the closure will be answered in turn from January 5, and replies may take longer than usual.
  • Post and parcels: mail and parcels addressed to us during the closure are held at the post office or carrier depot; delivery to us resumes January 5 onward.
  • Game masters (in-game support): 24-hour support continues as normal through the holidays.
