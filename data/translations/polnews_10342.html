A notice for Windows PlayOnline Viewer users on playing more safely.

Programs known as viruses and spyware exist on the internet; depending on the type, infection can occur merely by downloading a file or viewing a website. Infection can destroy data or leak personal information.

Representative precautions against such malicious programs:

  • Never use third-party tools not authorized by the company.
  • Do not access sites whose destination is unknown.
  • Do not carelessly download software from unknown sites.
  • Run Windows Update regularly and keep Windows current (see Microsoft's official site for details).
  • Update your web browser and other software regularly so you are always on the latest version.

To protect your valuable data, please manage your PlayOnline account strictly and keep your Windows and internet environment properly maintained.
