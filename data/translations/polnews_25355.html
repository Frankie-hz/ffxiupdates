An update on our Information Center's status for today, Friday, September 7, following the Hokkaido earthquake.

The power outage at the Information Center has been restored, but public transportation is not operating normally and staff cannot reach the office, so support is as follows.

  • Phone and chat support: suspended.
  • Email support: staff at other locations will respond as much as possible, prioritizing customers unable to use products or services; responses may take longer than usual.
  • Scope: support for Square Enix accounts, games (other than social games), online games, and web services. Game master support inside online games is not affected.

Further updates will follow as the situation develops.
