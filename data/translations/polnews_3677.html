Since the PlayOnline Viewer update of Wednesday, January 19, 2005, a problem has been confirmed where no response from the DHCP server can be detected when logging in to PlayOnline (POL-1040 error; "PlayStation 2" version only).

Our investigation so far has confirmed it occurs with specific routers. It has also been confirmed that connecting directly to the device provided by your ISP (e.g. an ADSL modem) without a router can avoid it in some cases. Our technical staff are continuing fix and verification work.

This problem was addressed in the version update performed from around 17:10 on Friday, January 21, 2005; see the news "PlayOnline Viewer Update (1/21)".
