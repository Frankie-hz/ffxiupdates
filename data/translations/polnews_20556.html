In response to the damage from the Tohoku earthquake of March 11, 2011, Square Enix is conducting relief support using Square Enix Crysta. Donated Crysta is converted to the equivalent amount of cash and donated to the Japanese Red Cross Society's Tohoku earthquake relief fund.

Details and donations: https://secure.square-enix.com/enqt/jsp/run/CRYSTADONATIONJ/HTML/

Donating requires a Square Enix account with a Crysta balance; Crysta can be charged from the Square Enix Account Management System.
