Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 53," with guest voice actress Emiri Kato, covering the November and December version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • The Voracious Resurgence story addition (November) (00:07:38): the stage moves to Windurst for a new tale, beginning with the NPC Kupipi at Heavens Tower. Like the first arc it is split into two halves with about a month of production between them, and the combined volume is about the same as arc one.
  • Ambuscade (November/December) (00:14:26): November - Volume 1 is Mamool Ja (revival); Volume 2 is Arcana appearing in relay fashion, and defeating a specific monster adds 100 Hallmarks to your clear reward. The night-time power-up gimmick on their special moves is disabled, so fight at any hour. New Hallmark exchange item: the Otter Axe (オータークアックス), vanity gear modeled on the Mamool Ja king's axe - graphics in the video. December - Volume 1 is Qutrub (revival); Volume 2 still in tuning.
  • Odyssey (November/December) (00:21:11): November adds Mog Segments, points earned many ways including defeating monsters/NMs, with Sheol C the most efficient source. More exchange items are planned, so banking points is reasonable if nothing appeals yet. December plans a boss area (tentative) with new equipment obtainable there, strengthened by repeat runs; it is being considered as high-difficulty content with a different flavor from Odyssey so far, possibly balanced around parties (not final).
  • Assist Channel (November) (00:27:17): a new chat channel for mutual player aid, bridging beginners/returnees and veterans (details in the online manual). The mentor system was adjusted alongside it: qualification changes and new mentor classes/knowledge ranks. The mute list privilege design was the most-debated point of the implementation - there is no obviously correct answer, so please share impressions from actual use.
