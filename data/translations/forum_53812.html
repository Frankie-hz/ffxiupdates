Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 38." We again broadcast ahead of the version update, focusing on an explanation of the March update. Here is a summary for those who missed it.

Version Update Highlights (0:03:38)

  • Dynamis Divergence (0:04:17): We toured the new area Dynamis-Jeuno [D] on live hardware, again using debug commands to explore without being attacked - see the video for route planning.
  • (0:15:38) Relic armor body and leg pieces become reforgeable. We showed Dragoon/Scholar body pieces and Warrior/Dark Knight leg pieces as samples. To upgrade a body piece to +2 you must have entered all four Dynamis Divergence areas on the job in question, and defeating each area's Wave 1 boss on that job unlocks +3. Progress already made in Dynamis-San d'Oria [D], -Bastok [D], and -Windurst [D] counts, so you only need to complete the new Jeuno requirements. Additional spoils from notorious monsters are also under consideration.
  • Ambuscade (0:20:08): This month's foes are Wivres and Moogles. Volume 1 spawns a single Wivre boss that weakens between winding up and executing its special move - landing enfeebles at that moment is the key. In the Volume 2 Moogle fight there are moments where deliberately NOT attacking a specific Moogle matters. The kill objective for the entry key item can be any experience-yielding monster, not Moogles specifically.
  • Upcoming Ambuscade updates (0:23:32): two years since Ambuscade's debut, so we covered what's next: the cape reinforcement UI gets the long-requested simultaneous material trading (demonstrated on hardware); a fifth augment slot with defensive options is being tuned for the April update; and some kind of new vanity equipment is under consideration.
  • Escutcheons (0:31:56): a new item, "cards," dropped by monsters in Adoulin areas; desynthesizing them yields illusory jewels (幻珠). Drop rates of existing items such as Carbuncle's Ruby and Elshimo stones will be raised.

15th Anniversary Corner (0:36:05)

  • The 15th Anniversary Records of Eminence reach their fifth and final installment, with objectives set in the Abyssea and Aht Urhgan areas - Besieged defense, fighting Maat, and other memories of those days.
  • (0:40:02) As a present for players who completed all five installments, an Omega mount - shown on live hardware in the video.

Fujito's /shout - Campaigns and Events (0:43:42)

  • Winter Relic Armor Reinforcement Campaign, Part 2: through around 23:59 on Saturday, March 31.
  • March 2018 Login Campaign: points earned through ~23:00 April 4, exchanged through ~23:00 April 9. Headline item: the Adaman Buckler (ダイアバックラー). Abyssea materials requested on the forums are also exchangeable.
  • Doll Festival: ran through ~23:59 March 12. Thanks for the many entries in the Doll Festival screenshot campaign; congratulations to the winners of the hina-dan display furnishing. Despite its large appearance it only has collision on its central 1x1 square - it can overhang at room edges, a deliberate choice to prioritize placement freedom.
  • An April Fools-themed community event is planned for April, apparently with equipment rewards - look forward to it.

The next Freshly Picked Vana'diel is planned for May. See you then!
