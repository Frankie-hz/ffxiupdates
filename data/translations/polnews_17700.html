Support arrangements over the year-end and New Year holidays:

  • Phone and chat support: closed Tuesday, December 29, 2009 through Sunday, January 3, 2010.
  • Mail: mail received during the closure will be answered in turn from Monday, January 4, and replies may take longer than usual.
  • Game masters (in-game support): 24-hour support continues as normal.
