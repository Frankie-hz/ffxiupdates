Following the application period held September 24 to October 1, 2003, the creation of the new Fairy world has been decided!

The migration will take place on Tuesday, October 7, during that day's scheduled server maintenance. After maintenance ends, characters that applied will have been moved automatically to the new world. All applicants will be migrated to Fairy.

An additional application window will be held so that final confirmation and sub-character applications can be made: Saturday, October 4, 0:00 to Monday, October 6, 0:00 (JST).

Cautions:

  • Character names: if names collide after migration, renaming may be required; there is no way to know in advance, and no cancellation after the deadline. If renaming is needed, the character's name appears in yellow on the character select screen - follow the on-screen instructions. Renaming does not lose friend list or other data.
  • Post and auction items: items in the Mog House post and auction consignments are automatically discarded at migration. Keep them on the character or in the Mog Safe beforehand.
  • Opened linkshells: linkpearls and linksacks vanish at migration. Linkshell owners receive a free replacement "unopened linkshell" automatically on first login in the new world.
