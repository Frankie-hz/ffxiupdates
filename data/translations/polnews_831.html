The GM call function on the PlayOnline Viewer - a means of handling inquiries about content other than FINAL FANTASY XI and about the Viewer itself - is now an official service.

Viewer GM calls let you contact a game master via text chat. Before calling, check the Q&A first; it holds many questions and answers about FINAL FANTASY XI and PlayOnline.

GM calls cannot handle: opinions/requests for PlayOnline; bug reports for games; contract inquiries; problems occurring before PlayOnline login; or known issues already listed. Use the appropriate mail form or the Information Center phone line for those. For problems during FINAL FANTASY XI play, call in game where possible. Calls judged to fall into the above may be closed; if yours was closed due to insufficient explanation, call again with clearer detail.
