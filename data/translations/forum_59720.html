Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 61," covering the July and August version updates plus recommended gearing content for beginners and returnees. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (July/August) (00:02:45): First a refresher on the concept - Ambuscade is where adventurers in full item level 119 gear practice for high-difficulty content and earn a rank-up in equipment: Volume 1 trains role coordination and gimmick handling; Volume 2, at lower difficulties, is an entry point even solo with Trusts. July's foes, both revivals: Volume 1 Goblins - windup-to-execution times on special moves extended overall, with tuning at Normal and above that highlights gimmick play; Volume 2 Antlions - the boss's damage cut was lowered so kills come roughly 20% faster. Details in the update notes. August, both revivals: Volume 1 Ramuh; Volume 2 Morbols - the dance-and-breath-loving Idol Morbol unit returns, with the Golden Bomb along for the ride.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (July/August) (00:09:55): an explainer for beginners/returnees, including a table of the missions needed to reach the Rhapsodies of Vana'diel final boss (the start requirement) - see the video. July is a production month with no story addition, but the Records of Eminence objective "Defeat the Curious Monsters!" gets additions. The ninth arc starts in August.
  • Empyrean armor reinforcement / new content "Sortie" (00:13:43): August brings Empyrean reinforcement and the new battle content Sortie. The required key item comes from an NPC in Leafallia and is reissued 20 hours after use. Entry is 1-6 players, Trusts allowed.
  • Other updates (00:20:48): Wings of the Goddess mission "On Thin Ice" (紫雲か、暗雲か) no longer requires an area change after failing the blowgun minigame. Domain Invasion Wyrms' Spike Flail: facing detection splits from front/back 180 degrees into 90-degree quadrants, narrowing Spike Flail to the rear 90 degrees. Bhaflau Thickets (Mamool Ja Staging Point island): new "Locus" monsters placed as Exemplar Point hunting targets - higher HP and strong resistance to area attacks, but slightly higher Exemplar Point yields. And for players who discarded certain ammo equipment (Chrono Bullet Pouch etc.), a one-time reacquisition mechanism arrives in August.

Recommended Content for Beginners and Returnees (00:28:57)

A goal-by-goal walkthrough of how a fresh level 99 should build up equipment, presented with content charts - see the video for the full rundown.

  • Reach item level 119 (00:31:42): recommended - spend Allied Notes from Wildskeeper Reives and similar on the item level 119 ancient armor sets.
  • Get Ambuscade equipment (00:32:35): explained from the Hallmark/Gallantry point system up. Exchanging for +1 Abjuration Tickets/Tokens is recommended since they yield one-stage-reinforced gear. Cape reinforcement was demonstrated with the Monk back piece Segomo's Mantle, showing how augments let you build differently-statted versions. Abdhaljs Seals were also covered - use them on high difficulties, during Gallantry campaigns, or when the Golden Bomb appears for efficient points.
  • Reinforce job-specific armor (00:44:46): material sources for Artifact (00:44:46), Relic (00:52:21), and Empyrean (00:58:52) armor, including skippable stages, walked through to the final upgrades - heavily condensed given the volume.
  • Legendary weapons (01:04:21): a quick overview of relic weapons and the other legendary lines. For the upcoming prime weapons, seeing the relic weapon storylines first will make them all the more enjoyable.
