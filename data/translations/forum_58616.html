Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (September/October) (00:05:45): Volume 1 is Poroggos (revival); Volume 2 is Cockatrices. The Cockatrice uses "Contagion," a special move that absorbs your enhancement effects, and takes more damage the more effects it has absorbed - so plan which buffs to let it drink. October: Volume 1 is Ahrimans (revival); Volume 2 is still in tuning but will feature a Halloween-flavored monster.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (September) (00:11:40): the fifth arc concludes in September, featuring the Automaton workshop members and NPCs from the Aht Urhgan missions and job quests. Some quest start points are far apart - see the update notes.
  • Other adjustments (00:21:24): Level-difference correction removed in the Arrapago Reef, Alzadaal Undersea Ruins, Beaucedine Glacier, and Palborough Mines - mainly affecting battles with level 99+ monsters, with little impact on ordinary fights. Fixed: FINAL FANTASY XI Config's simultaneous sound effects setting not applying for values of 13 or higher. The in-game new-PlayOnline-mail icon has been hidden (it remains visible within PlayOnline itself).
