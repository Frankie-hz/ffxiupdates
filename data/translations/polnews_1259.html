Lately, the townsfolk of San d'Oria, Bastok, and Windurst have been planning a rather unusual festival for children and adventurers alike: Halloween.

Do you know Halloween? Dressing up as ghosts to startle people, collecting treats and showing them off - that is Vana'diel's Halloween. One boy readies a skeleton costume; a certain guard crafts an Elvaan ghost outfit, hard at work researching how to squeeze treats out of adventurers!

The festival is nearly here - start preparing your very best treats!

Period: about four days, from around 0:00 Friday, October 31 to around 24:00 November 3.

To enjoy the festival: the three nations of San d'Oria, Windurst, and Bastok are the venues - roam the streets and find the costumed townsfolk! When you meet one, do not forget to hand over a treat. Handing out the same thing too often may bore them; rather than giving several to one person, enjoy the costumes and hand treats to many different people!
