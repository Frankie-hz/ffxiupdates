The "development information" forum beta-tested on the PlayOnline Viewer is now an official service. Unlike the beta, any FINAL FANTASY XI user can now register. Login restrictions apply only to posting; any PlayOnline member can read.

The forum publishes selected items planned or in development for FINAL FANTASY XI in advance, to gather opinions and host discussion among users. We look forward to active participation so that many users' voices can inform future development - come help shape Vana'diel.

Access it from the FINAL FANTASY XI top page via "Development Room" then "Development Information". Please read the Rules & Manners on its top page before participating. The PlayOnline extra service registration page (one of the registration requirements) is published under Member Information in Service & Support. The forum is also planned to become accessible from playonline.com in the near future.
