Lately, sightings of a mysterious giant creature have been pouring in near San d'Oria, Bastok, and Windurst. According to witnesses, touching a worn-out flag caused them to be attacked by a strange creature never seen before. The governments are rushing to identify it, considering the possibility of beastman retaliation!

Event start: Saturday, August 16, 2003, around 19:00 (JST)

Participation notes:

  • Doing "something" in the target areas grants event participation rights.
  • Participation rights expire after a set time; meet the condition again to regain them.
  • A level restriction applies while participation rights are active.
  • Being KO'd by the giant creature costs no experience points - but being KO'd without participation rights, or by anything else, does. Be careful.
  • The creature carries no items. Multiple parties and alliances can attack it simultaneously without Call for Help. Work together to defeat it!
