Level Cap and New Content

  • A Valkurm Dunes phase has been added to the title demo.
  • The level cap has been raised to 55. Equipment, abilities, magic, and other elements from level 51 up are now unlocked. To advance from level 50 to 51, you must clear the newly added quest "In Defiant Challenge."
  • High-level notorious monsters such as Behemoth have been added around the world. Besides fixed types that repop at regular intervals, some notorious monsters appear only under certain conditions.
  • More than 20 beastman-leader-class notorious monsters and many new drops have been added to the beastman dungeons.
  • An experience chain bonus system has been introduced, granting extra experience for consecutive battles. Defeating the next monster within a time limit after the previous one grants bonus experience, and the bonus grows with each consecutive kill up to a maximum of 300 points; the allowed interval shrinks as the chain grows. Only enemies rated "Even Match" or higher for you count, and defeating a "Decent Challenge" or weaker enemy in between resets the chain. (Chains are tracked per player, so members of the same party can differ.) Chain experience is displayed as a total including the bonus. Bonuses beyond 10 chain are calculated as a 10-chain bonus.
  • Continuing from last month, mysterious treasure chests have been added to nearly all dungeons, along with thief-specific chest abilities. Chests previously required the matching dungeon key, but thieves can now open them at any time using thief's tools or a living key, at the risk of springing a trap. Higher thief levels raise the success rate, but trap status effects also grow more severe with level.

Job Adjustments

  • Sneak Attack used from a support job no longer receives the DEX bonus. A bug where Sneak Attack would miss from certain directions has been fixed.
  • The thief job trait Treasure Hunter II has been added. The introduction of "Gil Steal II" and "Blast Attack" has been put on hold.
  • When singing on party members with an instrument equipped, bards can now layer up to two songs. Chorus effects from multiple bards have been changed: different songs of the same type stack, but the same song does not (Knight's Minne and Knight's Minne II stack; two Knight's Minne II do not). Along with this, the standalone effects of the Minne and Minuet lines have been slightly weakened.
  • Charm duration for beastmasters is now calculated from the level difference with the enemy and your CHR, with no random element - always a fixed duration. Charm no longer breaks on critical hits based on pet HP. The planned pet HP recovery catalyst was not introduced.
  • The pet command "Stay" has been added, and "Leave" can now be used from level 35.
  • Monsters that cannot be charmed can no longer be calmed either, and the failure rate of calming rises as enemy HP falls. Notorious monsters and some giant monsters can no longer be charmed.
  • Ranger-only ranged weapon skills have been added, TP can now be built with ranged attacks, and skillchains that connect from ranged attacks exist. Bow accuracy has been raised overall.
  • The skill requirements of the dagger weapon skills Viper Bite and Gust Slash have been swapped.
  • Skillchain possibilities have been added to some one-handed axe weapon skills: Raging Axe, Smash Axe, Avalanche Axe, Spinning Axe.

Magic System

  • The concept of a half resist has been introduced. Spells of non-weakness elements that previously simply missed now often land with halved duration or effect.
  • Sleep now has a fixed duration, halved under a half resist.
  • Magic Bursts have been introduced: connecting magic to a skillchain. Skillchains have always had attributes - 8 level 1 attributes and 4 level 2 attributes (higher levels undisclosed) - identifiable by the effect shown when the chain activates:
    • Level 1: Compression (dark), Impaction (lightning), Liquefaction (fire), Detonation (wind), Reverberation (water), Transfixion (light), Scission (earth), Induration (ice)
    • Level 2: Gravitation (dark/earth), Fragmentation (lightning/wind), Distortion (water/ice), Fusion (fire/light)
    • Level 3: ????...
    Landing a spell of the matching element with good timing on the skillchain yields a larger magic effect than normal - a Magic Burst. Since spells require casting time, synchronizing with the chain takes considerable technique. In the current version, to limit traffic and log scroll, Magic Bursts are not yet indicated by effects or messages and can only be confirmed by the change in damage numbers; work is under way to display them explicitly by the next version.
  • Three new white magic spells have been added: Invisible (blocks enemy sight), Sneak (blocks hearing), and Deodorize (blocks smell). They cannot deceive monsters that detect magic or life force, and many monsters have multiple senses. NPCs also do not react while you are invisible. These effects are removed by area changes or hostile actions, and Invisible also breaks when you act on party members.
  • The white magic spell Reraise is now obtainable, letting you pre-cast self-revival. It lasts up to one Earth hour; if you are KO'd during that time, it activates as if Raise had been cast. There is a small delay between KO and Reraise activating - don't rush back to your home point. A confirmation dialog now also appears before returning to your home point.
  • Experience loss on KO has been slightly reduced from level 25 up. A loss limiter on level-down was considered but not introduced.

World and Items

  • Level up/down effects are now visible to other players.
  • Magic and ability status icons have been subdivided and partly renewed.
  • A new full set of race-specific armor and quests to obtain it have been added, recommended level around 30.
  • Ribbon-type equipment is now visually represented as colored hachimaki so it can be seen on characters.
  • Harvesting (cutting grass) and logging (felling trees) have been introduced, and mining has been extended to new areas and items:
    • Harvesting: sickle - West Sarutabaruta, Giddeus
    • Logging: hatchet - Ghelsba Outpost, Jugner Forest
    • Mining: pickaxe - (ore) Yughott Grotto; (bones, fangs, etc.) Maze of Shakhrami, Tahrongi Canyon
    Mining and harvesting points can now more often yield items repeatedly at the same spot.
  • Gardening in the Mog House is now unlocked. Flowerpots and seeds are obtainable in various places (see the separate "About Gardening" page).
  • Macros have been expanded to 8 sets. Moving the cursor up/down in the macro edit menu switches palette sets, for a total of 160 macros. During play, hold L2+R2 (Ctrl+Alt on keyboard) and move the cursor up/down to switch sets.
  • Fishing rank certification tests have each been eased by one rank. Catches in Valkurm Dunes have been partly revised, and some tackle performance and fish data adjusted.
  • Shop prices, previously computed from your absolute fame in that nation, now also factor in your relative standing with other nations. Shop stock has changed in various places. Guild shop clerks' days off have been corrected.
  • New synthesis recipes have been added. Guild artisans now teach higher recipes sooner based on skill. Synthesis skill caps have been raised. Food effects have changed substantially.

Auction House Changes

  • Following the four nations' post-campaign cooperation, limited market information is now linked between national markets. Checking your listed items from another nation can now also clear expired and sold listings from other markets. The procedure for checking listed items has changed accordingly.
  • Listings are limited to 7 items per adventurer. When you list an item, what, for how much, and at which auction house is recorded in your listing list. Green names are items currently listed at that auction house; gray are items listed at another nation's auction house; yellow are items someone has won; red are items returned after expiring. For green items, pressing confirm lets you check past sales or cancel the listing. Gray items (other nations) cannot be canceled or checked remotely - you must visit that auction house. Yellow and red completed listings indicate the payment (if sold) or the item (if expired) has already been returned to your Mog House post; this reflects only the auction house's side, regardless of whether you have collected it. After checking each completed listing you must press confirm to clear it one at a time; until you do, it continues to occupy one of your 7 slots.
  • Canceled deliveries now remain in the delivery menu slot until you retrieve them, to prevent misdelivery. Select the canceled item and press cancel again to retrieve it.

Conquest

  • The crystal acquisition rate when participating in Conquest has been raised again.
  • Regional control tallying now weights battle efficiency more than before. Beastman influence has strengthened in several regions.
  • Individual conquest points during an alliance campaign have changed: in each other's territory, allied nations now earn 1x (was 2x); in the leading (rival) nation's territory, allied nations earn 3x (was 2x); the first-place nation is unchanged.
    Alliance campaign table - own nation 1st: 1x/2x/2x in 1st/2nd/3rd place nations' territory; own nation 2nd (allied): 3x/1x/1x; own nation 3rd (allied): 3x/1x/1x.
    Normal table - own nation 1st: 1x/2x/2x; 2nd: 2x/1x/2x; 3rd: 2x/2x/1x.
  • Signet duration now varies by rank and national standing, from 2 hours (rank 1, 1st place) up to 9 hours (rank 6, 3rd place), plus one hour per rank and per place below first (Earth time).
  • Trading unneeded equipment to the traveling merchant near an Outpost to raise your nation's regional points is now a permanent feature.
  • The fee for transferring allegiance between nations now varies with current conquest rankings.

  • The auto-translate phrase dictionary has been updated.
