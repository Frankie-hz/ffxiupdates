Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 37." Director Fujito was off this time, so Mocchi and I covered the February version update information together. For those who couldn't watch, or want to review the broadcast, here is a summary.

Version Update Highlights (00:04:17)

  • Dynamis Divergence: We toured the new area Dynamis-Windurst [D] on live hardware, exploring the whole area with debug commands keeping monsters from attacking (no combat). It may be useful for planning your runs - see the video. After internal review, the dark resistance of the boss monsters in each Dynamis Divergence area has been lowered, so Frazzle now lands effectively when combined with Elemental Seal.
  • (00:15:51) The next relic armor pieces eligible for reforging are the head pieces. We showed Black Mage and Ranger samples; like the other slots, they are tuned toward offense.
  • Ambuscade (00:17:51): This month's foes are Snolls and Dullahans. Volume 1 pits you against Dullahans - they may sound intimidating, but on Easy this is one of the gentler Ambuscades so far and can be attempted casually solo, while Very Difficult offers a real challenge. Per the designer, "attackers are the key," so work out the gimmick as you go. Volume 2's foes are Snolls, with many Bomb adds - deciding how to handle the Bombs (brute force or using the gimmick) is the point.

Fujito's /shout - Campaigns and Events (00:20:27)

  • February 2018 Login Campaign: the headline prize is the Lilisette statue. The campaign starts later than usual, so the point-earning period is shifted later as well.
  • Winter Relic Armor Reinforcement Campaign, Part 1: built around campaigns that help reinforce relic armor, such as the Incursion HQ campaign and the Dynamis shimmering-sand campaign. Part 2 is also planned.
  • New Year's Trust Campaign: running through the end of February - grab any Trust alter egos you've missed.
  • Valentione's Day: that season again; cute apron equipment and more.

Chat Corner - Q&A (00:27:33)

  • Ambuscade cape reinforcement UI: after reviewing play patterns, we plan adjustments where possible; timing is undecided, but we expect to start once Dynamis Divergence development settles down.
  • Wanting attractive Dynamis Divergence spoils: materials for new weapons will become obtainable as rewards; synthesis is planned to require the key item "gold book."
  • Wanting a reason to defeat NMs in Dynamis Divergence: NMs will get spoils distinct from regular monsters.
  • Frazzle not landing on Dynamis Divergence bosses: the dark resistance of the Dynamis-San d'Oria [D] and Dynamis-Bastok [D] bosses was lowered in the February 9 version update; the Dynamis-Windurst [D] boss uses the same value.
  • Too many item types crowding inventory: this has been a standing agenda item in dev/ops meetings and will be adjusted. Having NPCs hold reinforcement materials is difficult, but another approach is taking shape.
  • Reviving "Go! Vana'diel Day": no current plans to revive it on other or multiple days. "Current" because events reusing its system may happen - we'll consider it after reaching the May 15, 2018 goal.
  • Keeping the Trust "Cornelia" available: Cornelia's powerful stats were only possible because she was time-limited, and the plan was premised on using one limited Trust slot for a fixed period. To keep our options open for that slot, Cornelia will depart as scheduled with the May 2018 version update.

The next Freshly Picked Vana'diel is planned for March. See you then!
