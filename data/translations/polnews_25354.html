Due to the Hokkaido earthquake that struck in the early hours of Thursday, September 6, 2018, part of our Information Center is unable to operate, and support is affected as follows.

  • Phone and chat support: suspended.
  • Email support: partially suspended.
  • Scope: support for Square Enix accounts, games (other than social games), online games, and web services. Game master support inside online games is not affected.

Further updates will follow as the situation develops. We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.
