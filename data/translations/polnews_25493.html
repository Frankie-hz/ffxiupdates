From Friday, January 11, 2019, in Docomo payments made on smartphones with a Square Enix account, devices that do not support TLS 1.2 (Android OS below 4.4 and iOS below 5) connecting over Wi-Fi will no longer be able to access the Docomo payment screen. Connections over the Docomo network (3G/LTE) are not affected.

Customers using Docomo payment in that environment should consider updating their device OS to the latest version.
