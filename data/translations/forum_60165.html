Hello, this is Matsui. Thank you for watching "Freshly Picked Vana'diel 63," covering the November and December version updates. Summary below.

Version Update Highlights

  • Ambuscade (November/December) (00:01:46): November, both revivals - Volume 1 Iron Giants, Volume 2 Mantises. Iron Giants: to reward gimmick play, the damage multiplier against them is raised while only one is active and lowered while both are - and on Difficult, some special moves' power and status durations were reduced (they had mistakenly matched Very Difficult). Mantis: boss HP cut about 20%, and its mode-change move "Mantodean Reprisal" reworked - the damage reduction granted now scales higher with the number of buffs it strips, and takes effect from even a single buff. Details here. December, both revivals: Volume 1 Meebles; Volume 2 Harpeias, tuned so you'll be having a song battle, with the customary Golden Bomb gimmick planned too.
  • The Voracious Resurgence (November) (00:06:56): arc ten begins, set in Oldton Movalpolos (cutscene screenshots shown). The quest starts with Gumbah in Bastok, and requires clearing the Chains of Promathia epilogue. Prime weapon stage 2 arrives - stage 2 weapon names were revealed on video. Important: progressing the story from December onward requires one prime weapon advanced to stage 2 (with a damage value); a stage-2 weapon suffices through the story's conclusion, but shields and instruments alone cannot advance the story, so prepare a prime weapon of another type. Apologies for the late notice.
  • Sortie (November) (00:10:50): status of congestion/stability work - several optimizations shipped (e.g. player-action calculations), and the layer system has been identified as the bottleneck; investigation and testing continue on a fundamental fix. A boss NM was added to the October sector - far stronger than existing NMs, prepare thoroughly - along with its treasure coffer. (A community report that a particular NM stayed spawned longer was investigated: no such change was made.) Empyrean +3 reforge eligibility flags are now visible as key items per job; if you already earned a flag, speak to the Sortie entrance "Diaphanous Transposer" at the Kamihr Drifts to receive it.
  • Other updates (00:16:58): Mog Ledger [30] added for Empyrean +3. Master Level cap raised to 50 - the final raise. Bibiki Bay gains Locus monsters (golden crabs and others) after requests for a hunting ground under open sky; slightly stronger than Crawlers' Nest [S]. The Trust "Matsui-P" returns from November 2022 through the May 2023 update.
