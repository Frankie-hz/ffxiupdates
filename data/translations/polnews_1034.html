PlayOnline will offer three expanded mail services under the name "PlayOnline Plus". A beta test begins today, with beta tester recruitment now open.

  • Character name address: use your in-game character name as your own mail address (currently FINAL FANTASY XI names), e.g. xxx@yyy.ffxi.pol.com where xxx is the character name and yyy the world name. Up to five can be configured; they act as additional sender addresses on your PlayOnline Mail account (the number of mail accounts does not increase). Useful when you do not want to reveal your main address, or when an address containing your character name is friendlier to correspondents.
  • Mail forwarding: forward PlayOnline Mail to the addresses you normally use (up to two destinations).
  • Mail blocking: block unwanted mail by subject and by address, up to 20 entries each.

Recruitment closes per-service once suitable numbers are reached. Becoming a beta tester requires contracting/purchasing the content ID of the desired service, but PlayOnline Plus is free during the beta (planned to last about three months). Apply from "PlayOnline Plus" under Topics in the PlayOnline Viewer.

PlayOnline Plus is in development and mail-related problems may occur; report them via "Report a Problem" on the PlayOnline Plus top page.
