Today we changed how credit card payment processing for Square Enix accounts connects with the payment agency. The effects on customers:

Cardholder name entry: after the system change, the cardholder name must be entered when updating the card information registered for service account payments, and when entering card information at payment time. Cards registered before the change need no name entry until updated.

3-D Secure version change: the identity verification service has been upgraded from 3-D Secure 1.0 to the more secure 3-D Secure 2.0. Depending on your card, payment errors may now occur; if so, configure 3-D Secure for your card, or consider another card or payment method. If no errors occur, nothing needs to be done. For 3-D Secure setup and support status, contact your card company.
