The reception hours of the PlayOnline GM call beta have been extended from the previous 11:00-19:00 to 24 hours a day, so that players who could not apply due to the limited hours can now use it.

Use it for problems the Q&A cannot resolve, or when troubled by nuisance behavior from other users. For problems during FINAL FANTASY XI play, make the GM call in game where possible. Calls judged inappropriate may be closed; if you believe yours was closed due to insufficient explanation, call again with clearer detail.
