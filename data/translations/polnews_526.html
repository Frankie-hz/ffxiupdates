A handy tool exclusively for PlayOnline community sites is here! Functions previously available only on playonline.com can now be viewed and used on community sites:

  • Event information banner - shows FINAL FANTASY XI and JanHoRo event information, linking to the related sites.
  • Recommended community site links - sites picked by our online community team staff.
  • Random community site links - random fan sites from the 500+ registered on the PlayOnline community site list.
  • Latest PlayOnline news - the five latest news headlines, including maintenance information.
  • PlayOnline Q&A search - quickly find answers to questions about PlayOnline.

Several skins themed on PlayOnline and FINAL FANTASY XI are available and more will be added. Setup is simple: just add the source to your page's HTML - no downloads or difficult configuration. Registration on the community site link list is required.
